MARKET DESK REBORN 3.3 — WHAT CHANGED?

OPEN IT
Start Market Desk, then choose Home. The new What changed? section sits above
your existing home modules. Overview's older Daily briefing remains available.

START COMPARING
The first briefing shows saved coverage without calling earlier records new.
Choose Set my starting point after reading it. Later, Reload saved briefing
compares loaded records with that point. Mark this snapshot reviewed replaces
it explicitly. Reopening the app never silently clears the comparison.
The saved point's time is the snapshot's read time, not an exchange timestamp.

WHAT THE TABS MEAN
Price records: changes between two actual saved Finnhub quote endpoints for
the same symbol/currency. Both timestamps stay visible. This is a raw quote
comparison, not a complete time-window return or a split/dividend-adjusted
investment return. A quote newly loaded today can itself be older than your
review; that is labelled. Missing/older quotes, equal-time revisions, unknown
currencies and unsupported ratios are withheld, not turned into zero changes.
Headlines: new to this saved comparison, not necessarily newly published.
Publication and retrieval dates are separate. Company tags are provider feed
scopes and are not independent relevance or causation checks. Links open the
publisher; article bodies are not copied or generated.
Filings: completed saved SEC filings, not forecast earnings dates. Date-only
filing records never imply an exact filing time. An earlier filing loaded now
is identified as earlier activity.
Calendar: the next 14 calendar days plus changed details of previously saved
nearby events. Confirmed/estimated/cancelled statuses remain explicit. Provider
earnings remain estimates; date-only records stay date-only. A cancelled date
can appear as a revision and is excluded from Upcoming.
Coverage: actual quote times, headline retrieval dates, cached issuer coverage,
missing sources and truncation warnings. No records does not mean no activity.

WATCHLISTS
Each set of company symbols has its own review point. Lists with identical
companies share it, regardless of name/order. Up to eight most recently
reviewed company sets are retained; older comparison points can expire from
this small cache. Switching sets does not delete notes, projects or prices.

UPDATING SOURCES
Opening/reloading this briefing only reads saved local data. The normal app's
existing price collection can still run while the app is open and connected.
Update one company, inside the folded controls, explicitly uses the existing
Finnhub quote/profile/news requests, shared pacing, caches and plan limits.
A restriction/failure preserves saved data and does not mark it reviewed.
It does not refresh SEC accounts. Use Company Overview's existing SEC update
when needed. Missing news/calendar access is not bypassed with AI guesses.

SAVE / OFFLINE / EXPORT
Review points are part of the existing V3 private workspace and full research
backup. They retain compact quote endpoints/source IDs/event details, not a
complete market database or permanent read-history. Download briefing (.json)
exports the currently loaded comparison for inspection. Settings' full research
backup is the supported restore route; complete price databases/keys remain
separate. Opening older versions can discard unknown new fields, so keep a
3.3 backup and stop older servers before upgrading or downgrading.
Saved briefings work without internet while the local server runs. New data
needs connectivity/provider access. No monitoring, collection, training or
continuous learning occurs while the Mac/app is off. No paid OpenAI or new
AI model is required; this briefing does not use AI to invent explanations.

LIMITS
Up to 60 watched companies; 200 recent saved headline references, 200 saved
filing references and 80 nearby event records enter a comparison. News retains
the existing seven-day saved-headline window. The calendar uses saved records
from a seven-day lookback through thirty days ahead; Upcoming is fourteen days.
Warnings identify capped subsets. Review-point storage is bounded to 512 KB.
Missing or disappearing records do not establish inactivity or cancellation.
