MARKET DESK — USING THE STOCK SCREENER

1. Open Markets > Stock Screener.
2. Start with My watchlist, or choose the 221-company directory.
3. If figures are missing, leave number filters empty, select up to five stocks
   and press Refresh selected. Finnhub must be connected in Settings.
4. Enter a maximum positive P/E, minimum growth or minimum margin. For example,
   P/E up to 50 finds stocks with usable, positive ratios no higher than 50;
   it does not mean those stocks are cheap or suitable investments.
5. Check dates & sources before comparing companies. Retrieved today does not
   mean the financial reporting period is today. Missing figures remain dashes.
6. Switch to Price-change map for the same shortlist. Only eligible quote pairs
   appear. The quote time and eligible count stay visible.
7. Open a chart or ask Intelligence for further research. Unwatched stocks are
   added first, within the existing watchlist limit.

AGE LIMITS
Quote eligibility defaults to three days to allow closed-market weekends.
Financial snapshot eligibility defaults to seven days. These are user-chosen
retrieval/quote-age policies, not guarantees about financial reporting dates.
Choose 15 minutes if you need recent quotes. A provider may supply older quotes
or restricted fields even after a refresh; the app does not invent replacements.
Old numbers can still be inspected in the table but do not pass active filters.

WHY DO I SEE NO MATCHES?
The app does not download data for the entire directory automatically. Clear
active numeric filters, select companies and refresh a small batch first.
Widen age limits only after checking source dates. Unknown values are not zero.
Stocks outside the directory may have unknown listing currency; those cannot
pass USD price filters. Ratios and percentage change need no currency conversion.
Negative or zero P/E may be displayed if the provider reports it, but cannot
pass the Maximum positive P/E filter. Revenue growth and net profit margin use
TTM (trailing twelve months) fields; different companies' periods can differ.

MAP INTERPRETATION
The change is (recorded quote price / provider previous close - 1) * 100.
It is not necessarily the change today or over the last 24 hours, and it is not
a dividend-adjusted investment return. Red, green and neutral tiles also carry
arrows and numbers. Every tile has equal area. Browsing groups are editorial,
not official sectors. Neither colour nor passing a filter is a buy/sell signal.

FILTERS AND DOWNLOADS
Hide filters gives more room for results; the active criteria remain visible.
Clear filters resets criteria. Filter presets are browser-local, capped at ten,
and are not part of the research JSON backup. Download their JSON before
changing browsers or ports. An import replaces the saved bank after confirmation.
CSV downloads only the current matches and includes filters, original UTC
source dates, links and age-eligibility flags. It can include explicitly labelled
old values when no numeric filter requires their exclusion.

PRIVATE DATA
Saved quote/profile/financial snapshots use the existing private database.
Keep that folder on upgrade. They are excluded from research JSON backups;
CSV is a readable source copy. Credentials are never included in these exports.
No collection, refresh or model training continues while the Mac is off.
