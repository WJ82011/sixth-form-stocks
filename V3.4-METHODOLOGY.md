# V3.4 Analyst Lab: research method and limits

This is original application code informed by public professional research
methods. It is not a copy of a bank's private system, a Wall Street consensus
feed or evidence that Market Desk can outperform professionals.

## Research basis

Business forecasts can be built from selling prices, volumes and operating
costs, with scenarios when outcomes are uncertain. This informed the business
driver worksheet. [CFA Institute: Company Analysis — Forecasting](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/company-analysis-forecasting)

Understanding the business precedes selecting and applying a valuation method;
sensitivity and the expectations embedded in a valuation matter. V3.4 provides
explicit conditional multiples alongside the existing DCF tool; it does not
supply market-consensus expectations or automatically reverse-engineer a live
share price. [CFA Institute: Equity Valuation — Applications and Processes](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/equity-valuation-applications-and-processes)

Analyst ratings and conflicts require care; a professional-looking output is
not itself a reliable recommendation. [SEC: Analyzing Analyst Recommendations](https://www.sec.gov/files/investor/pubs/analysts.htm)

Take-Two reports net bookings separately from recognised revenue. This is why
launch inputs distinguish net receipts and revenue recognition instead of
multiplying a retail game price directly into reported revenue. No GTA sales,
price or timing assumptions are preloaded. [Take-Two SEC quarterly filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/946581/000162828026054870/ttwo-20260630.htm)
[Company investor relations](https://www.take2games.com/en/ir)

## Business engine: analyst-driver-v1

The current saved SEC book must have annual revenue and operating profit/loss
for the same fiscal start, end, monetary unit and accession. Displayed amounts
are converted to millions of that book's native currency; US GAAP and IFRS
books are kept distinct. The first supported annual book is used, with its
basis and currency visible. Custom concepts and missing lines are withheld.
No figures are silently treated as zero. Refreshes use the retained explicit,
paced SEC path. Calculation rejects changed or mismatched baseline inputs.

The latest annual filing is a scale reference, not trailing-twelve-month data
or a current run rate. The next twelve months are a modelling horizon, not a
claim that an old annual period describes current trading. Restatements may
change latest facts; these are not a historical availability archive.
Operating profit is unadjusted and can include exceptional charges. Complex
businesses, banks, insurers, changing segment mix or acquisitions may require
a more appropriate model. The user supplies the cost classification.

Let R be baseline revenue, O baseline operating profit/loss, f the affected
share of revenue, v the variable-cost share of baseline revenue, and p, q, c,
g the price, volume, core-unit-cost and fixed-cost changes as fractions.

- Baseline fixed cost = R − Rv − O; negative results are rejected.
- Core revenue = R[(1−f) + f(1+p)(1+q)].
- Core variable cost = Rv[(1−f) + f(1+q)](1+c).
- Fixed operating cost = (R − Rv − O)(1+g).
- A launch contributes annualized units × company net receipts per unit ×
  (13 − first selling month)/12 × recognition share × (1−cannibalisation).
- Launch variable cost = launch revenue × its explicit cost share.
- Operating profit = core revenue + launch revenue − core variable cost −
  launch variable cost − fixed operating cost.

Units in millions and per-unit receipts produce revenue in millions. The
launch assumes an even run rate after launch, not an estimated sales curve.
Cannibalisation discounts incremental recognised launch revenue; it is a
simplified haircut, not a detailed displaced-product or cash-flow model.
Confirmation is required that core changes exclude separately modelled launches.

Optional enterprise value uses an explicitly justified EV/Sales or
EV/Operating-profit multiple and a positive denominator. Equity value deducts
user-supplied net debt and other senior claims. Per-listing-unit value divides
by diluted underlying shares and multiplies by underlying shares per listing
unit/ADR. Currency/share/ADR alignment must be explicitly confirmed. Missing
capital inputs withhold per-unit value; a non-positive equity residual does
not become a usable share-price target. No FX, share count, net debt forecast,
taxes, discounting, terminal value or probability is inferred. Multiples and
capital assumptions are not independently verified. There is no comparison
with a live quote, no implied upside recommendation and no trading instruction.

Sensitivity changes one Base-case input by up to two percentage points,
clipped at its supported bounds, holding other inputs fixed. Labels do not
create probabilities; a warning flags profit results inconsistent with the
Downside/Base/Upside order. Plausibility still needs human judgement.

## Evidence, saved records and the local assistant

Evidence distinguishes reported facts, guidance, news and user assumptions,
with dates, source links/descriptions, summaries and separate implications.
Links do not fetch or independently verify articles. The nearby source desk
shows at most six recent saved company-list headlines and saved FRED series.
Company-news lists can include irrelevant stories. Observation dates are not
release dates; macro series can be revised. These are context, not historical
supervised signals or a causal explanation for a price movement.

Evidence can inform a human choice of assumptions but is not automatically
converted to a growth rate or numeric price impact. There is no fake historical
backtest of current news, product launch claims or revised financial statements.
Dated point-in-time financial/news archives would be needed to test such a
model without information arriving before it was actually available.

Complete saved analyses retain frozen baseline inputs, all three cases,
evidence and an actual creation timestamp. Existing IDs cannot be overwritten.
Evidence/filing dates cannot postdate the record. Read-only review recomputes
from its frozen inputs and original horizon, with no new provider request.
Records are user data, not cryptographically audited source documents; edits
to an imported backup cannot independently prove source authenticity. Draft
forms are unsaved until a complete analysis is saved. Full workspace backups
include saved analyses; malformed data cannot replace a valid private backup.

The retained local language model receives the latest active saved company
worksheet as A1–A4 evidence, explicitly labelled conditional user assumptions.
Scenario passages are not linked as if the SEC made those forecasts. The
assistant can explain supplied inputs/results; citation, number and source
checks remain active. It cannot invent a quotation or fill missing company
facts from model memory. It is not being fine-tuned by these worksheets.
No paid OpenAI service, new language-model download or cloud fallback is added.
The bounded evidence packet prioritises the requested scope: saved worksheets, news or chart records. Earlier one-close model passages are labelled separately from the new five-close experiment. No new semantic-accuracy guarantee is made for generated AI answers.

## Price experiment: price-factors-ridge-v1

This separate genuine fitted ridge regression uses verified saved Massive
split-adjusted daily closes, not imported or generated prices. Current New York
session labels are excluded conservatively even after that day's close. At
least 220 closes are required, with no unsupported gaps, duplicate labels,
extreme returns or invalid prices. At most 500 closes are used. Saved adjusted
history can contain later adjustments; it is not a point-in-time exchange archive.

The target is log return over five observed closes. Features available at each
anchor are previous-close return, 5/20-close momentum, 20-close return volatility
and 60-close drawdown. SPY previous-close return and 5-close momentum are added
only if at least 220 contiguous recent company dates align exactly. Otherwise
the same five company factors remain. No macro/news/product/fundamental feature
is secretly inserted, and no language model is trained by this regression.

The first test follows a 60-close lookback, at least 100 labelled training
examples and the five-close label delay. At each anchor only training labels
ending by that anchor are eligible. Means/scales are fitted on those training
rows; expanding ridge uses a fixed 0.1 covariance penalty. Test anchors are
five observed closes apart, so evaluated return windows do not overlap.
Earlier labels are available to later fits, as they would be after completion.
There is no favourable hyperparameter search over these displayed results.
The final fit uses all completed labels and displays learned weights per
training standard deviation, which are associations rather than causal effects.

Errors are mean absolute five-close return errors in percentage points. The
baseline predicts zero return (unchanged price), on the same test cases.
Relative skill is 100 × (1 − model MAE / baseline MAE); zero baseline error
withholds this ratio. Negative skill is displayed as failing to beat the
baseline. Return errors are not a strategy's profitability, win rate or live
execution outcome; spreads, fees, dividends, FX and trading rules are excluded.

A central 10th–90th-percentile historical log-residual range requires at least
20 previous holdout errors. Sequential range coverage uses only residuals
already observed before each coverage test, with the evaluated count displayed.
The current range uses completed holdout errors; it is not a guaranteed future
probability interval. Samples can be small. Data older than ten calendar days
withholds the current point estimate and range while retaining historical tests.
The estimate is anchored to the last saved close, not a live quote; no future
calendar target is invented. None of this establishes a persistent trading edge.

## Privacy, cost and originality

The update is local, retains prior tools and uses existing provider budgets.
Reading/calculating/training from saved records makes no new market request.
SEC updates remain explicit; Finnhub/Massive plan permissions, quotas, delays
and missing coverage still apply. No paid data tier or API is required by this
update, and no scraping or quota bypass is added. Keys and personal records
are excluded from the release. Collection and training stop when the local
server or Mac is off. No external animations, paid designs or proprietary bank
code were copied. Read V3.4-VERIFICATION.md for the actual checks and limits.
