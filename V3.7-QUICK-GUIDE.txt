MARKET DESK 3.7 — SIMPLE GUIDE

OPEN
1. Export a full research backup in your old version's Settings.
2. Stop the old version's Terminal window with Control+C.
3. Extract MarketDesk-Reborn-v3.7.0-Mac.zip.
4. Open Start Market Desk.command inside the extracted folder.
5. Keep Terminal open and visit http://127.0.0.1:8765.
Keep ~/.marketdesk-reborn — your existing keys, research and prices live there.
If needed, type python3 followed by a space in Terminal, drag the new server.py
into Terminal and press Return. Python 3 is required. Do not open index.html
directly. Use the normal macOS approval flow if it asks about the launcher.

THE NEW HEATMAP
Open All tools → Market Heatmap. Choose 1 day, 3 days or 1 week at the top.
These mean 1, 3 or 5 COMPLETED US trading sessions, not rolling calendar days.
Weekends and scheduled market holidays are skipped. The two exact comparison
dates stay visible. The free feed excludes the current New York date, even
after the close; its previous-day data becomes available after midnight ET.

Your existing Massive connection is used automatically on opening the map.
The first load needs six daily market files, usually about two minutes if no
other requests are using the allowance. Each file covers the directory, so you
do not have to request 221 companies individually. Follow the progress bar.
The 1-day map becomes usable after the first two files, 3-day after four,
and 1-week after six. Missing or invalid individual bars stay gray.
If a connection is missing, open Data connections and save your free Massive
key privately in Settings. Finnhub alone does not fill the new period map.

Pause stops future requests; a request already in progress may finish.
Load all company history resumes from the next missing file. Closing the app
preserves progress. Reopening resumes unless you paused or a provider error
stopped the update. A new New York date starts a new six-file generation so
prices from different adjustment runs are not deliberately mixed. Older usable
maps stay visible with their actual dates while a new set is being collected.
An interrupted generation is only resumed within that same New York date.
No data is collected while your Mac or server is off.

COMPANIES OPEN DIRECTLY
Click a tile to open its company chart with exactly the heatmap dates.
No Add & open step, watchlist membership or free subscription slot is needed.
A small price line and the closing price are already on each available tile.
Select any other chart range to leave the fixed heatmap comparison. Older
short histories may not cover longer ranges: use Update daily prices or your
existing CSV import for those ranges. Intraday history still needs actual
recorded timestamped observations; daily bars are not passed off as intraday.

Stock Browser's primary button also opens directly. The company picker lists
all directory companies. Follow this company is OPTIONAL: it adds only the
companies you want to your personal watchlist. Its existing 60-stock limit and
live-feed subscription limits remain. The open Overview chart takes one temporary
live slot, including for an unfollowed company. Leave Overview to release it.
Choose 1hr or 24hrs to see the recorded intraday feed. Fixed heatmap charts
continue to display their completed-session dates.
When Finnhub is connected, opening a company requests its profile, quote and
news through the existing budget/cache, at most once per ten minutes per
company in this browser session. Missing or restricted information is still
labelled unavailable. Other tools retain their own explicit refresh controls.

OTHER V3.7 IMPROVEMENTS
Search companies, choose a browsing group, sort biggest rises/falls first,
or focus on your own watchlist. Map options contains grouping and CSV export.
Period, sorting, grouping and scope are remembered in this browser. They are
not added to the full research backup. Search and group remain temporary.
The CSV includes actual start/end dates, prices, returns, currency and source.
The map is a defined shortlist, not the entire stock exchange. Tile area is
layout only. Groups are editorial, not official financial sectors.

PRESERVED FEATURES
The earlier market-wide value cards, sourced observations import/export,
notifications and company-size map are in expandable sections below the main
map. Daily price notifications still use watched Finnhub quote crossings,
not these end-of-day period returns. All 22 workspaces, research, local
AI, forecasting, portfolios, recovery and previous source rules remain.
No paid OpenAI service, new model download or continuous-learning claim.

DATA AND STORAGE
Provider prices are saved in the existing private database and are not bundled
in the release ZIP. Their license/access depends on your account. The new
heatmap store keeps three recent date generations, with six files each, and
only directory companies plus up to 60 extra watched symbols. The existing
chart store keeps at most 550 daily bars per company. No raw whole-market
response archive is kept on disk. Existing saved data/models are separate
from the small application ZIP. Keep your private data folder backed up too:
full research JSON backups do not contain provider price caches. Heatmap CSV
is a report export, not a way to restore its full six-file historical store.
