MARKET DESK REBORN 2.8 — YOUR NEW DESK

OPEN IT
Stop the older version with Control+C in its Terminal window. Unzip this
release and double-click Start Market Desk.command. Keep Terminal open.
The app opens at http://127.0.0.1:8765. Do not open site/index.html directly.
Your existing private data folder is reused; the download contains no keys.

FIND YOUR WAY
Overview: choose a company, a chart period and your preferred amount of detail.
Companies: browse, search or manage your watchlist, including Take-Two (TTWO).
Workspace: your research notes. All tools: all 19 research and settings pages.
Daily briefing and Detailed workspace remain on Overview. Chart expansion,
CSV imports, forecasts, reports, alerts, live tape and research are retained.
The original sidebar and price strip are available with Original sidebar in
Customise. The simple layout keeps the scrolling strip out of the way.

MAKE IT YOURS
Customise opens appearance and motion options:
- Quiet, Balanced, Cinematic or Off.
- Dark, light or system appearance; five accent colours.
- Rounded surfaces, spacing and simple navigation or original sidebar.
- Dissolve, glide or gentle depth; three speeds; glow, glass or no background.
- Fine-tune strength, pointer response, hover, reveals and chart crossfade.
Changes save automatically and travel with your full research JSON backup.
Existing edition, chart size, contrast and price-strip controls remain in
Settings. Screen animations there stays in sync with Enable animation here.

ACCESSIBILITY AND DATA
Your Mac's Reduce Motion setting overrides animation. Off stops background
motion and makes navigation instant. Background animation pauses in hidden
tabs. Only the chart's opacity can fade; recorded values are never animated
into different prices. Missing records, source dates and uncertainty remain.
A new design does not add data access or strengthen forecast reliability.
The existing free data limits and optional local Ollama setup are unchanged.
Nothing records or learns while this Mac is off. No paid OpenAI is required.
