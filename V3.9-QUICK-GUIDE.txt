MARKET DESK 3.9 — QUICK GUIDE

OPEN ON YOUR MAC
1. Stop the old version with Control+C in its Terminal window.
2. Unzip MarketDesk-Reborn-v3.9.0-Mac.zip.
3. Open Start Market Desk.command in the extracted folder.
4. Keep Terminal open. If needed, visit http://127.0.0.1:8765.
Keep ~/.marketdesk-reborn to carry forward your private research, data and
connections. Python 3 is required. Use normal macOS approval if prompted.

THE PURPLE LINE
Open Overview and choose a company. Choose a daily range such as 1Y, enable
Show research forecast and click Focus on purple line. The curve starts at
the last observed close and uses a closer price scale. Choose Show full chart
and range to see the price history and illustrative uncertainty band together.

The centre uses recent and longer trends that fade at different speeds. When
enough earlier periods resemble the current one, it also draws on what those
periods did next. Sideways scenarios can be flat. Price Forecasts shows past
tests against an unchanged-price baseline. The purple band is an illustrative
range. Figures are scenarios and actual prices can move differently.

LIVE TAPE
1. Open All tools → Live Tape. Search for any of the 221 directory stocks.
2. Choose Trade line, 1-minute candles or 5-minute candles.
3. Choose a time window. The table can filter by minimum reported trade size.
4. For past records, choose Review last recording, pick a Saved date (UTC),
   and move Review time. You can also drag the chart to move along that date.
5. Choose Back to live to return to the rolling window.

Candles show the first, highest, lowest and last prices in each time bucket
using the positive-size trades Finnhub sent and this Mac recorded. The bars
below show captured size. The size filter applies to the table. Export saves
the visible chart window. Replay shows the newest 4,000 retained records per
loaded window, with dates listed from the retained local history.

Pause display freezes the visible time and sample while the app can continue
recording. Stock Browser still controls your watchlist. Opening a directory
stock in Live Tape uses an available slot in the shared 30-symbol stream budget.
Saved review works with a previously populated local database. The live tape
needs Finnhub, an active connection and incoming trades to collect new prints.
