MARKET DESK 3.6 — QUICK GUIDE

OPEN
1. In the old version, export a full research backup from Settings.
2. Stop the old version's Terminal window with Control+C.
3. Extract MarketDesk-Reborn-v3.6.0-Mac.zip.
4. Open Start Market Desk.command inside the extracted folder.
5. Keep Terminal open and visit http://127.0.0.1:8765.
If macOS asks about the launcher, use Finder's Open action and the normal
macOS approval flow. If needed, type python3 followed by a space in Terminal,
drag the new server.py into the window and press Return. Python 3 is required.
Do not open site/index.html directly. Do not delete ~/.marketdesk-reborn:
this is where existing keys, saved prices, research and local data live.

MARKET VALUES
All tools → Market Heatmap. Each available card shows its two reporting dates,
currency, scope and original source. US data currently compare the latest two
public quarterly endpoints checked on 6 November 2026. These are a bundled
snapshot, not an automatically updating subscription. A quoted-value change
is not the amount of cash paid into or withdrawn from the market.
Other countries show unavailable until you import comparable data.

Under Add sourced market-value observations, download the CSV as a template.
Use market,currency,date,capitalization,unit,scope,source columns. Supported
markets/currencies: US equities/USD, UK equities/GBP, Euro-area equities/EUR,
Japan equities/JPY, Hong Kong equities/HKD. Units: millions/billions/trillions.
Use at least two past dates, identical coverage text and the same currency
for each market. Public source URLs must use HTTPS without a query or login.
Confirm that the endpoints are comparable and that you may use the data.
Import replaces custom capitalization observations only, after validation.
At most 1,000 observations and 500 KB per file. User-provided claims are not
independently verified. Comparisons use the latest two supplied dates.
Save this CSV separately: the full research JSON backup does not include
this new dataset. It also remains in your existing private database folder.

NOTIFICATIONS
In Market Heatmap, expand Price-move notifications and wider stock coverage.
Enable the checkbox. Fresh watched quotes crossing +5% or -5% versus an
unchanged provider previous close can appear at the top right. There is one
alert per company/session/direction, no initial-record replay, and no alerts
from stale observations. These are recorded quotes, not confirmed live trades.
New matching publisher headlines have dates and links; relevance and claims
are not independently verified. The selected watched company's news is
checked at most once every two minutes while enabled, through existing quota
and cache controls. Other companies use cached news. Toggle resets off on
reload. Keep the app open. No notifications or learning while Mac is off.

CHARTS
The latest quote, selected chart range and saved completed-session comparison
can cover different dates. Each is labelled separately. A rising intraday
slice can still be below yesterday's close. Matching-session intraday charts
show a previous-close reference where available. Longer ranges retain their
actual range return. Use Update daily prices for actual missing provider
bars; access still depends on your free plan. No prices are interpolated to
pretend to cover an unobserved hour. No graph is reversed to match a headline.

STOCKS AND RESEARCH
Stock Browser and heatmap now include 221 listings. Directory membership does
not guarantee quote/history access. Gray tiles mean missing usable prices.
Add companies to your watchlist for tape and notifications; existing watchlist,
quote-request and stream subscription limits remain. Company-specific research
question starters complement the earlier V3.5 research and forecasting tools.
