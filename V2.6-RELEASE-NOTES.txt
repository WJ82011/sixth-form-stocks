MARKET DESK REBORN v2.6.0 — VISUAL MARKET & SMOOTH NAVIGATION

A more visual way to understand the saved market data, with detailed tools
still available. Original rounded black-and-blue styling; no competitor assets.

MARKET HEATMAP
Open Markets > Market Heatmap. Your watchlist appears as coloured company tiles,
optionally grouped into browsing categories. Green is up, red is down, blue is
unchanged and gray means data is missing, incomplete or outside your chosen
age limit. Colour strength follows the size of the reported change. The quote
time is visible on each priced tile. A short, count-based plain-English summary
explains the available picture, with a coverage count and latest quote time.

Choose the 166-company directory to explore more companies. Unknown changes
stay gray rather than being filled in. These are editorial browsing groups,
not official sectors. Tile area is layout only, not market capitalisation or
index weight. This is a bounded saved-quote view, not an exchange-wide feed.
The existing screener's filtered Price-change map also remains available.

CLICK TO UNDERSTAND
Click a stock for a simple explanation card: direction, recorded price, quote
time and a short explanation of comparison with its provider previous close.
Source details are expandable. Open its chart or ask Intelligence when you
want deeper research. Unwatched stocks are clearly labelled Add & ... and the
existing 60-stock watchlist limit is respected. Escape or the close button
returns keyboard focus to the stock tile, including after a background update.

LIGHTER, MORE GUIDED PAGES
Screener filters now start tucked away; current criteria stay visible and the
full controls still open with Show filters. Its summary labels are simpler.
The longer Overview chart commentary is under Understand this chart. Source,
coverage and forecast labels remain visible. Signal Overview has an Open
heatmap shortcut. Both editions retain all existing tools and preferences.
On phones, extra search/group controls sit under Map options to give the map
more room. Applied search/group criteria remain visible when options close.

SMOOTH MOTION
Sidebar groups expand/collapse over 220 ms. Quick changes interrupt the prior
animation without leaving a group stuck or clipped. Pages fade and move very
slightly over 260 ms; stock cards have a short opening transition. These are
interface effects only: price values are not animated into invented points.
Both the saved motion preference and OS Reduce Motion are respected. Native
keyboard disclosure behavior, current-page cues, parent alert counts and
focus handling remain. Full-screen charts bypass page transforms so their
viewport positioning and rounded corners remain correct in Professional mode.

DATA AND COST
Opening/filtering the map reads existing local snapshots. Explicit price
refresh requests at most eight quotes, through the existing caches and shared
provider budget. It does not bulk-download all 166 companies. No new model,
paid AI service, chart library, remote font or visual dependency is installed.
All original 18 tools remain; Heatmap is tool 19 within the same six-entry menu.

UPGRADE
Stop the older Terminal with Control+C, unzip this version and open
Start Market Desk.command. Keep ~/.marketdesk-reborn. Existing keys, snapshots
and saved research remain in that private folder. Ollama is used as before
when you choose AI. See HEATMAP-GUIDE.txt and V2.6-QA-REPORT.txt.
