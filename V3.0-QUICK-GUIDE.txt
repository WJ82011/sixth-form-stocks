MARKET DESK 3.0 — SIMPLE GUIDE

Use Home to open charts, your practice portfolio, calendar or research.
Arrange your home screen changes the card order and hides unwanted cards.
All tools always opens the complete collection. Command+K also finds tools.

COMPANY ACCOUNTS
Open Company Overview, choose a company, then Update expanded SEC accounts.
Scroll to Go deeper into the accounts. Select the reporting basis/currency,
statement and period. Exact value & inputs reveals original concepts and
filings. Calculated quarters and TTM are labelled calculations. Custom rows
and CSV imports are separate, unverified research. Start sourced report saves
a named report using the selected book; review it before sharing.

PRACTICE PORTFOLIO
Add a hypothetical deposit dated before your trades. Record buys, sells,
fees, dividends and splits with their sources. Never enter real orders here.
Use saved USD quotes uses existing dated records; it does not request prices.
For other currencies enter the price, explicit USD conversion and FX source.
Add marks dated on each checkpoint date to compare two snapshots. Missing
or stale marks prevent a complete portfolio total. Editing old trades can
invalidate checkpoints. The legacy importer assumes zero fees and requires
an explicit starting cash date; your original V2 records remain available.

CALENDAR AND ECONOMY
SEC filing history records completed filings. Earnings requests depend on
your Finnhub plan; provider dates are estimates. Add your own sourced event
and revise it if dates change. Calendar export creates a local .ics file.
FRED cards show observation periods, not live readings or predictions.

DISCOVERY AND COMPARISON
SEC listing search is a directory, not a promise of prices for every listing.
Add a supported symbol to your watchlist, then load its data explicitly.
Fundamental screening uses saved annual US-GAAP/USD records only.
The new heatmap sizes boxes using eligible saved company market caps.
Chart comparison needs saved Massive daily bars for the selected companies;
only shared actual dates are used. Imported history remains in original tools.

BACKUP
Settings exports/imports the complete research workspace including V3 state.
Keep a backup before upgrading or replacing records. Research JSON does not
contain credentials, model weights, price databases or local AI excerpts.
A private local workspace backup is also queued after saved changes.
