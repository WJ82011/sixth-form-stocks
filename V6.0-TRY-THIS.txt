FIVE THINGS TO TRY IN MARKET DESK 6.0

A new person has not done these yet. The notes below are from the developer’s own pass. Difficulties found in that pass were fixed where the records allowed it.

1. Find Take-Two
Type Take-Two in the search box, or choose Gaming companies in the workspace menu. The chart should show TTWO.

2. Compare two companies
Open Comparison. Choose two companies and two dates, then Compare saved records. Save the comparison if you want it in a research pack.

3. Create an alert
Open Price Alerts. Pick one company, one rule and one level. This step was not submitted during the developer pass, so it is still pending as a real alert.

4. Investigate a move
Open Stock Screener, run or reopen a saved scan, and click a company. Home should open on that company with Review this move under the chart. A missing SPY close is stated. It is not filled in.

5. Reopen saved research
Open Research Reports and reopen the saved edition. The page should say it was not rebuilt. The journal entry keeps the prices from the day it was saved.
