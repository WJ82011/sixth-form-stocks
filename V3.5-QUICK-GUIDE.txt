MARKET DESK V3.5 — A CLEARER RESEARCH WORKFLOW

OPEN THE UPDATE
1. Export a full research backup from Settings in your current version.
2. Stop its Terminal window with Control+C.
3. Extract MarketDesk-Reborn-v3.5.0-Mac.zip.
4. Open the extracted folder and double-click Start Market Desk.command.
5. Keep Terminal open and visit http://127.0.0.1:8765.
   If Terminal prints another address, use that address. Closing the older
   app first lets you keep port 8765 and the same browser workspace.

If the launcher does not run: type python3 and a space in Terminal, drag the
new server.py into Terminal and press Return. Python 3 is available from
python.org. You do not need Node.js to run Market Desk.
Keep your existing ~/.marketdesk-reborn folder and local AI installation.
The ZIP contains no keys, private research, recorded prices or model weights.

FIND V3.5
All tools → Market Desk Intelligence → Research Desk at the top.
The section buttons jump to company evidence, what-ifs, the forecast journal
and the existing local AI assistant. No extra sidebar entries are added.

RESEARCH A COMPANY
Enter a symbol such as TTWO and load saved accounts. Update SEC accounts
explicitly if needed. The desk separates what is known, what to investigate
and what is missing. Read the filing dates, saved quote time, matched headlines
and upcoming events. Headline matching can miss relevant stories and include
irrelevant ones. A calendar date is not a guaranteed announcement.

TRY A WHAT-IF
1. Complete the shared business assumptions and explain them. Use the existing
   reference button to start three unchanged-business cases, then edit them.
2. Open Build a what-if. Choose a business input, your hypothetical percentage
   change and the case to explore. No numerical change is invented for you.
3. Optional: ask the installed local AI to explain mechanisms, evidence and
   risks. It uses the local model selected in Intelligence below. Accepted
   drafts keep supporting excerpts; meaning still needs human review.
4. Preview this what-if. Your analysis has not changed at this point.
5. Apply reviewed what-if only if appropriate. The chosen case receives your
   numerical assumption and an explicitly labelled assumption record.
6. Compare all cases, review the chart and save a completed analysis.

The new chart starts with reported operating profit/loss and shows mechanical
contributions from prices, volume, costs and launches. Price is applied before
volume; their interaction is assigned to volume. This is a model explanation,
not proof that those factors caused an actual market move. Detailed valuation
and launch figures remain available inside each case card.

RECOVER UNFINISHED WORK
Analyst forms save a recovery copy in this browser as you edit. This includes
incomplete numbers, case inputs, unfinished source-entry text and what-if form
inputs. Open Draft recovery and explicitly restore the company you want.
Restoring a draft does not turn it into a saved analysis. Recalculate and review
it before saving. Original annual source inputs are retained; changed current
accounts require a fresh review before calculations are accepted.

Saving a completed analysis keeps its recovery copy, including source text
that you have not submitted yet. The two records serve different purposes.
Recovery supports up to ten companies within a conservative storage budget of
about 0.4 MB. Download drafts before removing older recovery copies. Recovery
copies join your full research backup. Use the same browser and local address.
Private backup writes are queued; the browser recovery copy is saved first.

If browser storage fails, keep the page open and download the current form.
If recovery data is damaged, its raw contents are retained for download; it is
not silently replaced with an empty library. New private backups are refused
rather than overwriting the last good backup with damaged recovery data.
Standalone draft/recovery downloads are audit copies, not separate import
formats. Restore through the normal full research-backup importer when needed.

RECORD AND CHECK PRICE FORECASTS
The prediction journal is separate from business scenarios and historical
backtests. It records a freshly calculated five-session price estimate using
saved provider closes. It needs at least 220 usable closes and the most recently
completed scheduled session, retrieved at least 15 minutes after that close.
Update daily history through the existing chart controls when needed.

Recording is supported for Nasdaq/NYSE USD listings in the local directory.
The bundled regular-session calendar covers 2026–2028, including published
holidays and early closes. Unexpected closures are possible. A forecast is
withheld when calendar coverage or sufficiently recent final data is missing.
Only one forecast per company and starting session is allowed; it cannot be
replaced with a better-looking result after another model run.

Later, press Check saved outcomes. When all five specified future session
closes are available, the journal shows actual return, model error and the
unchanged-price error. Missing dates are not replaced by later dates. Revised
prices can withhold scoring, including possible corporate-action adjustments.
No outcomes are invented before they happen. Historical model tests and newly
recorded future results are labelled separately.

Archived journal entries remain in the scores. Both all scored records and a
chronologically selected non-overlapping cohort are reported. Small samples
and correlated companies do not establish an advantage. No fees, spreads,
dividends, currency conversion or trade execution are modelled.

IMPORTANT BACKUP DISTINCTION
Recovery copies and saved business analyses join your full research JSON
backup. Forecast journal receipts/outcomes are stored in the private price
SQLite database, like other recorded market data. Download journal creates a
separate audit copy; it is not a journal restore/import format. Preserve your
private data folder when moving or upgrading the app. Up to 200 journal records
are retained; none are automatically deleted to make room.

Nothing collects prices, writes forecasts or learns while this local server
or your Mac is off. On return, actual provider history can be loaded and used
to check previously recorded forecasts. No paid OpenAI service, cloud fallback
or new model download is added. Read V3.5-METHODOLOGY.md for technical details.
