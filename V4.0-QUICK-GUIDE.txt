MARKET DESK 4.0 — QUICK GUIDE

1. Stop any older Market Desk server with Control+C in its Terminal window.
2. Unzip MarketDesk-Reborn-v4.0.0-Mac.zip. Keep your ~/.marketdesk-reborn folder.
3. Open Start Market Desk.command. The local address is http://127.0.0.1:8765.
4. Use All tools → Learning & offline for collection and forecast checks.

KEEPING DATA UP TO DATE
You can close the browser tab and leave the Market Desk Terminal running.
To run without that Terminal, stop it first and open Enable Background at
Login.command in the extracted folder. This registers a per-user macOS
service. Keep the folder in place. Use Disable Background at Login.command
before moving the folder or upgrading. The launcher uses your existing Python.

The Mac needs to be awake and online to collect live data. The server resumes
provider requests when the connection returns. A sleeping or powered-off Mac
cannot collect live trades. During an outage you can reopen saved charts,
tape, news and research at the local address while the server is running.
Downloaded historical bars can recover supported periods after reconnecting.
Any missing live trades remain gaps.

Finnhub trades stream for up to 30 subscribed stocks. Quotes rotate across
the watchlist within the provider budget. The collector also refreshes
company and market news, Bitcoin and Ethereum snapshots and 24-hour history,
Massive daily closes and heatmap files. Exact coverage depends on your saved
provider connections and plan. Financial statements and other research
sources still use their own refresh controls.

THE LEARNING LAB
Choose a directory company and train from saved prices. If you need more
history, use Update daily prices & train with an active Massive connection.
The report uses consecutive split-adjusted closes and tests 1-, 5- and
20-session outcomes. The model uses close returns, momentum, volatility and
drawdown. The longer horizon needs more history to produce enough test cases.

Each past fit sees only outcomes available at that date. The lab compares
the learned model against an unchanged price in an earlier calibration block
and a later test block. A learned scenario needs to win both checks.
Otherwise it shows the unchanged-price baseline or asks for more data.
The illustrative range reflects earlier errors and has no guaranteed odds.
The original purple chart remains a separate research scenario.

The collector evaluates watched companies in rotation when the server is
running, including from saved data during an outage. When the data revision
stays the same, the report reuses its saved training result. It trains on
daily price history. Headlines and economic events are shown separately
because a historical record of what was known on each date is needed to
test their predictive value.

DATA
The ZIP contains source and guides. Existing saved data and credentials
remain in your private local folder. A backup of that folder preserves the
database. Quit the server before making a manual copy of the whole folder.
