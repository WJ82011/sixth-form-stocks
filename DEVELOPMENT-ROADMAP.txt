THIS RELEASE: v2.5
Stock screening has begun with the 166-company directory plus watched symbols,
explicit finite-value filters, age controls, saved criteria, source inspection
and CSV export. Whole-market financial screening remains a later expansion.
The proposed market-heatmap idea has begun as a clearly labelled, equal-tile
price-change map of the available shortlist. This is not a sector or market-
capitalisation-weighted heatmap. These are bounded, usable first versions.

NEXT PRIORITY: FINANCIAL STATEMENTS LAB
Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow from primary SEC filings for
eligible US issuers. Must handle issuer/ticker mapping, units and currencies,
fiscal periods, amendments/restatements, annual versus quarterly facts and
source filing links before feeding figures into valuation or AI. No statement
numbers should be invented or inferred from a company summary. This was
explicitly postponed in v1.9 and has not been implemented in v2.5.

THEN: RICHER PRACTICE PORTFOLIO ACCOUNTING
Cost basis, realised/unrealised returns, concentration and comparison with a
properly matched benchmark. Dividends, FX and multi-asset support need dated
sources and accounting rules. The current USD hypothetical ledger remains;
no real trading or brokerage connection is planned as part of this work.

THEN: EVENTS AND ECONOMIC SOURCES
Source-linked earnings/company events with timezones, confirmed/estimated
status, revisions and missing-data handling. Data access must be checked rather
than assuming paid calendar endpoints are free. Extend official macro sources
with observation dates and frequencies; never use AI to invent an event date.

LATER EXTENSIONS
Broader screening only when licensed/free data coverage permits; verified
market-cap currency/units; source-aware cap weighting; more chart comparisons;
custom dashboard layouts. New tools should use the existing sidebar groups
rather than restoring a long flat menu. Each release needs its own tests and
clear limitations. No release dates or paid-data promises are implied here.
