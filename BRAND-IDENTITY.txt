MARKET DESK / CREST — BRAND IDENTITY v1.6.3
===========================================
DESIGN
The Market Desk Crest is a custom, abstract, double rising-peak symbol.
Read together, the two forms suggest an M; their shifting heights evoke
price movement, evidence and the perspective needed for financial research.
It is intentionally not an Apple, Samsung or other existing company logo.

APPLICATION
* White/ivory on deep navy; no gradient, shadow, framed tile or decoration.
* Vector: site/brand-mark.svg (transparent backdrop, 56 x 56 viewBox).
* Browser icon: site/favicon.svg; Home Screen icon: site/apple-touch-icon.png.
* Repeats in Signal sidebar, Professional sidebar and terminal masthead.
* It remains sharp when zoomed and when viewed on a Retina Mac display.

NOTE
The design was made for this project, but it has not received a formal
trademark similarity search. Conduct an appropriate search before commercial
brand registration or public deployment.
