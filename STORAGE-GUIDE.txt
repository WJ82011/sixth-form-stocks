EXCLUSIONS AND GROWTH
This is a file-size estimate, not an exact whole-computer disk-use total. It
excludes Python, the browser, their caches, operating-system files, older app
versions, backups/exports and future research data. Finder may report different
allocated sizes, and APFS compression/clones can change physical disk use.
A larger or additional local AI model would increase the total substantially.

Saved data grows with followed companies, captured trades, excerpts and drafts.
The app caps observations at 5,000 per stock, captured trade events at 18,000
per stock and daily bars at 550 per stock. Local AI retains up to 100 source
documents (20 per stock) and 100 accepted drafts across stocks. Other notes,
alerts and downloaded/exported files can add more. SQLite may keep freed pages
for reuse instead of immediately shrinking its file. There is no fixed total
storage promise. Keep the private ~/.marketdesk-reborn folder on upgrade;
it contains the saved data and settings the app needs.
