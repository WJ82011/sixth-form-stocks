MARKET DESK v1.7 RESEARCH REPORT GUIDE
==================================

Start with Apple (AAPL) or another company that you already follow.
Collect evidence from the company's recent annual report (investor relations),
quarterly reports, official filings and source-linked Finnhub profile metrics.
Write what you think, not what the software thinks.

A HIGH-QUALITY SIXTH-FORM PORTFOLIO REPORT SHOULD CONTAIN
1. A single clear economic or business question.
2. How the company earns revenue and what drives demand.
3. Real financial figures with exact fiscal period, source and currency.
4. At least one comparison with a genuine peer.
5. A reasonable alternative thesis and risk factors.
6. What new evidence would change your view.
7. A conclusion that distinguishes established facts from judgments.

HOW TO EXPORT A PRINTABLE PDF
Open Research Reports > select and load a report > Print / Save PDF.
In the macOS browser print dialog choose PDF > Save as PDF. The PDF is
generated locally by your browser; Market Desk does not send research
to an external PDF service. Also export Markdown for an editable copy.

WHAT TO AVOID
- Do not claim a provider P/E/ratio is dated today unless verified.
- Do not imply that a price snapshot is a verified streaming tick.
- Do not interpret purple Research Forecast scenarios as certain prices.
- Do not include private API keys in source citations or exported reports.
