MARKET DESK 6.0 — ENGINEERING REPORT
14 November 2026. Local only. No new paid data source.

WHAT WAS ADDED
Prices, filings, news and calculations can now carry a stable reference. A reference records the kind of evidence, the company, when it was published, when this Mac retrieved it, and the period it describes. An investigation can be traced from its finding back to those references.

A linked chart board on Company Comparison shows up to four US companies. Each company keeps its own saved closes. A shared date range uses New York sessions and leaves a gap where a close is missing. Price and percentage views are separate. Percentage means “from the first saved close in this window,” not a dollar price. The board can be saved and reopened without being rebuilt. Dated events and chart notes already saved for that company are attached. Moving averages stay on the main chart.

Intelligence can propose, run, cancel and resume “Investigate TTWO's latest completed weekly move.” The return is calculated by the existing move review. If a number is not in the saved closes, the step says so and does not invent one. A later change to those closes can produce a new investigation. The original is kept.

Model checks are stored one by one, including checks that do not beat an unchanged price. The unchanged-price baseline stays active unless the calibration block and a later block both support the candidate. Forecasts that were already issued are not replaced.

Settings can inspect missing sessions, duplicate Massive bars, large jumps and stale saved closes. Nothing changes until a repair is requested, and the only repair collapses duplicate Massive bars. It can be undone. User-imported history is not written.

WHAT THE APP DID WITH SAVED TAKE-TWO RECORDS
The latest completed week on this Mac was 9 November 2026 to 13 November 2026. The saved closes were 207.4 and 227.82, which is 9.85%. SPY had no saved close on those two dates, so no benchmark number was produced. Ten saved items were listed. The finding says those items do not explain the move. The price reference for 9 November has a different publication time from the time the bar was retrieved.

The four-chart board for Take-Two, Apple, Microsoft and Nvidia over those five sessions returned in about 5 milliseconds. Each company had five saved closes and no missing session in that short window. The saved board reopened with the notice that it was not rebuilt. The crosshair is the same fraction of each chart; in a shared range that is the same session.

The Take-Two session model had 34 later tests and remained on the baseline. One ledger row was saved. No issued forecast was replaced.

Inspection of Take-Two found 11 duplicate Massive sessions. Nothing was repaired. Those rows are still the original saved bars.

HOW IT WAS TESTED
Nine focused tests cover a paused investigation, a missing price, a new version after a changed close, a shared board with a gap and a dated event, 3,200 test points in under a second, a failed model candidate, an interrupted repair that rolls back, and backup restore. The full offline suite then passed 639 tests, three times. In the browser, the steps were shown before the return, the four charts drew with a linked line, and the approved comparison page remained underneath.

REMAINING LIMITS
A new person has not tried these screens. The linked board does not draw moving averages. Crypto is not mixed into the equity sessions. The 11 duplicate Take-Two bars were only reported. Repair is available in Settings and was not used. A headline is still not a cause. The baseline remains the active Take-Two method.
