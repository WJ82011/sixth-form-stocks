HOW TO OPEN IT

CHART AND REPLAY
Move across the chart for the recorded time and price. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, or use Pin point, Reset view and Back to live. Arrow keys pan, plus and minus zoom, 0 resets, and Home returns to live.
Open Live Tape and press Load saved session. Play, pause, step or change the speed. The chart, trades and timed events stay together. Prices and events stay hidden until the replay clock reaches them, including the chart scale. Date-only events are listed separately and marked timing unknown. Replay does not arm or trigger price alerts.
