# V3.5 method and boundaries

V3.5 builds on the original V3.4 business and price engines. It adds a guided
research desk, explicit what-if approval, a mechanical profit explanation,
recovery copies and prospective forecast receipts. Earlier tools remain.

## Prospective prediction journal

`forecast35.py` uses the existing `price-factors-ridge-v1` engine. It is still
an experimental daily-price regression, with the features, expanding-window
backtests, fixed ridge penalty and limitations described in
[V3.4-METHODOLOGY.md](V3.4-METHODOLOGY.md). It is not a trained news/fundamental
model and is not evidence of a persistent trading advantage.

Creation happens on the local server at its actual current clock time. The
client supplies a symbol, not a forecast, issue time, anchor or future outcome.
The source is the validated saved Massive daily-bar cache, excluding imported
and generated series. Per-bar retrieval times in that existing table are
seconds; the journal converts them explicitly to milliseconds. Bars must have
been retrieved at least fifteen minutes after the applicable scheduled close
and no later than the creation/check time. This is a conservative availability
check, not an independent guarantee of provider finality.

A supported Nasdaq/NYSE USD listing in the dated local directory is required.
A fresh forecast must start at the most recently completed scheduled session,
with at least 220 usable historical closes. Earlier provider history can be
used for training; prospective session selection is restricted to the bundled
2026–2028 calendar. Five exact future regular-session dates are recorded.
Calendar holidays and early closes follow the public
[NYSE schedule](https://www.nyse.com/trade/hours-calendars), checked 6 November
2026. Emergency closures or later schedule changes can leave a target
unscorable. No unknown calendar years or missing sessions are guessed.

A receipt freezes the issue time, listing currency, starting close, future
dates, original model summary/weights/range and supplied company/benchmark
price windows. Some supplied prices may be unused because of feature lookback,
label delay or benchmark alignment. A consistency hash covers the original
receipt. A unique company/anchor constraint prevents replacing that forecast
with another run. There is no retrospective forecast import or user backdating.
The private database retains at most 200 records; new creation is refused at
capacity rather than pruning inconvenient history.

Checks use actual saved closes for the five recorded future dates only, after
the last target session and the final-data buffer have elapsed. Missing dates
remain missing. Return error is an absolute percentage-point error against the
same target used by a zero-return/unchanged-price baseline. A first completed
assessment is frozen separately, with an integrity hash and scores that must
reconcile to its captured prices. The original forecast receipt is unchanged.

Changes to overlapping frozen training prices or captured outcome prices
withhold current scoring, including possible split/correction/adjustment
changes. Original assessments remain in the private database. Once older
prices leave the bounded cache, an already frozen assessment can remain with
an explicit inability-to-recheck label. This is an as-recorded local history,
not a continuously re-audited point-in-time exchange archive. Hashes detect
inconsistency; they do not independently authenticate a user's Mac or clock.

All scored records, including archived ones, contribute to the aggregate.
A separate cohort is selected chronologically per company, accepting another
record only when its anchor is at or after the prior accepted target date.
These windows do not overlap within a company, but companies and returns can
still be correlated. Small samples, return error and range coverage do not
establish tradable profitability. Fees, bid/ask spreads, execution, dividends
and FX are excluded. Business cases are never scored as price predictions.

The journal lives in a new table in the existing private price database.
Its JSON audit download includes original inputs and the current assessment
view, not a restore mechanism. View-only `archived` and `evaluation` fields
are outside the original receipt hash. Full research JSON backups cover
research records/recovery copies, not the price database or journal table.

## Scenario assistant and numerical approval

The optional assistant uses the existing installed local language model and
existing job, citation, source-excerpt and digit-verification paths. A bounded
H35 source describes business-model mechanics and any user-entered hypothetical
percentage, explicitly as an unapproved assumption. Current saved company
sources supply context. Questions are kept within the existing engine limit.
The language model does not supply or silently apply numerical growth rates.

The user chooses the model input, magnitude and case. A preview runs the normal
server calculation against the exact saved SEC baseline without changing the
analysis. Applying requires an explicit action and a still-matching draft.
It adds a labelled user assumption, not reported company guidance. The user
then compares and saves a completed analysis separately. A changed draft or
company invalidates a pending preview. Delayed AI replies are scoped to their
original company; the complete accepted AI source snapshot is retained through
the existing saved-AI-draft system.

No model download, fine-tuning, paid OpenAI service, cloud fallback or new
external provider request is introduced by asking for help. Source/digit checks
do not establish semantic truth, completeness or causal interpretation. Gaps
in supplied excerpts do not prove that an entire original filing omits a fact.
Manual what-if previews remain usable without a local language model.

## Profit explanation

`research35.bridge` reconciles each valid business case to operating profit.
Using the V3.4 notation, the sequence is:

1. Start with reported annual operating profit/loss O.
2. Price contribution: R f p.
3. Volume contribution: R f (1+p) q − R v f q.
4. Core unit-cost contribution: −R v [(1−f)+f(1+q)] c.
5. Fixed-cost contribution: −(R−Rv−O) g.
6. Launch contribution: recognised launch revenue minus its modelled cost.
7. End at the same case's calculated operating profit/loss.

Price precedes volume, assigning their interaction to volume. These mechanical
attributions depend on that order and are not observed causal effects. Native
currency/millions, negative profit and missing valuations retain their earlier
meaning. The original annual scale reference is not converted into invented
trailing-twelve-month data. The chart includes a text alternative and internal
horizontal scrolling on narrow screens. Valuation/launch details remain in
expandable case cards. No new charting library or third-party visual assets
are needed.

## Recovery and backup behaviour

Recovery stores incomplete form state separately from immutable analyses,
under a dedicated browser key. Source identity/baseline, all case inputs,
shared/capital forms, unfinished evidence-entry text and assistant form inputs
are captured. Recovery does not preserve generated chart results as if they
were verified calculations. An explicit restore reopens the original source;
subsequent calculation checks it against the current saved SEC facts.

Browser writes are immediate; private research-backup writes use the retained
queue. Up to ten company copies are supported, with a conservative 400 KB
browser budget and a 450 KB normalized backup limit. Unknown fields are removed
and imports are validated before existing browser records are committed.
Partial fields are allowed as drafts but cannot bypass full analysis validation.
A completed analysis save keeps its recovery copy, including unsubmitted text.

The local store checks record revisions and delayed restores before replacing
work. Detected cross-window changes stop an overwrite; these checks are not a
claim that all browser storage operations are transactional across windows.
Storage failures keep the last good copy and report the unsaved state. The
current form can be downloaded. Damaged JSON is retained for raw download and
is not replaced by a blank library during startup. Damaged recovery data blocks
new full backup writes/exports rather than overwriting a valid private backup
with an empty stand-in. It may require manual repair or an explicitly imported
valid backup after its raw contents have been preserved.

## Guided company evidence

The research desk uses saved annual statement coverage, dated quotes, matched
headlines, filing records and nearby calendar events. Headline selection uses
simple title/ticker aliases, not a trained relevance or sentiment classifier.
It can miss useful articles or include irrelevant ones. Full articles are not
downloaded. Dates, revisions, provider plan limits and unsupported concepts
remain visible; no future sales or launch result is filled in. Existing free
Finnhub/Massive budgets and explicit refresh controls remain unchanged.

All collection, generation, recording and checks require the app/server to be
running. On reopening, newly downloaded genuine provider closes can settle
older recorded targets; no background activity while the Mac is off is implied.
