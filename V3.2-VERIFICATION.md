the existing Mac application. The original notes, reports, charts, statements,
Intelligence, portfolio, alerts and saved data remain available.

## Data and recovery boundaries

Projects store the author's research. Links are not automatically fetched;
notes are not verified facts or new AI training data. Chart snapshots copy
browser-plotted data, retain source labels and remain user records, not an
independent attestation. Imported chart sources remain unverified. Snapshots
show up to the latest 600 displayed prices; their count and actual dates are
visible, with no backfilled market periods or forecast prices.

Linked reports remain separate editable records. Deleted/missing or wrong-company
reports are withheld, not reconstructed. Per-project JSON includes report
copies for portability, while Settings' full research backup is the supported
whole-workspace restore route. It includes captured project prices, but not the
complete market databases or credentials. The older release guides describe
their historical baselines; use V3.2-QUICK-GUIDE.txt for this release.

Saved project/item/watchlist edits carry their opening revision. Latest browser
state is read before mutation and a change during an in-flight save is rejected.
This protects the tested same-browser multi-window workflow; it is not a
multi-user cloud database or a promise of simultaneous editing across computers.
Research/library byte and count limits are explicit. Permanent removal requires
archiving and confirmation and needs an earlier external backup for recovery.
No hardware, provider uptime, investment accuracy or AI semantic guarantee is
implied by these regression results.
