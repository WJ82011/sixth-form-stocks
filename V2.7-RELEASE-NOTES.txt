MARKET DESK REBORN v2.8.0 — A CLEARER DAILY WORKSPACE

DAILY BRIEFING
Overview opens with your watchlist's available price comparisons, its largest
recorded rise/fall, dated company cards and linked general headlines. Counts
are for your watchlist, not a market index. A genuine zero change remains zero;
missing or over-age data stays labelled. Quotes over 15 minutes old are marked;
comparisons older than three days are excluded. News is dated publisher context,
not an asserted cause of price movements.

COMPANY & CHART
Choose a watchlist company for a short description, its existing interactive
chart, source dates and company-name/ticker title matches from its headline
feed. Sixteen default companies have original, manually written background
summaries, including Take-Two/Rockstar. Other companies use a saved provider
industry when available; a missing business description is not generated.
More company details and the original research pages remain available.

CLEARER PRICE HISTORY
Range buttons show saved, partial, outdated or import-needed status before you
select them. These are date-span hints, not a guarantee of every trading day.
The chart's actual dates, gaps and endpoint-change summary remain visible.
Saved genuine Massive daily bars are readable offline without a key or fresh
provider request. This does not download missing history or make old prices live.

Your price history reviews the selected source plus a saved-file library.
Download an imported CSV, open the simple sourcing guide, or add/replace a file.
Use provider history removes only that ticker's imported copy and restores the
provider path. Original downloaded CSVs and provider bars are untouched.
Import metadata is migrated at startup and updated atomically, so rereading
the briefing does not decode full multi-year files.

THE DETAILED WORKSPACE REMAINS
The original Signal/Professional layouts are available through Detailed
workspace. Heatmap, screening, AI/model lab, news, notes, alerts and portfolio
tools remain in the same sidebar groups. The same chart canvas moves between
layouts; there is no duplicate price feed, chart engine or local AI model.
Keyboard operation, Reduce Motion and the app's animation preference remain.
Phone and fullscreen chart layouts were checked in Professional Edition.

PROVIDER REQUESTS
Opening/rereading the briefing reads saved snapshots. Explicit briefing refresh
requests up to eight quotes plus general headlines, rotating through the oldest
checked stocks. Company refresh requests one quote, one profile and company
news; it does not request financial metrics. Both use the existing caches,
shared Finnhub budget and provider backoff. The app's existing scheduled quote
cycle continues separately. New briefing reads do not add a provider timer.
Massive Basic remains within the existing conservative two-year date window.
Full 5Y/10Y history still needs an appropriate longer CSV or eligible provider
access. No paid OpenAI, extra model or paid data subscription is required.

START
Stop the old Terminal process with Control+C, extract this ZIP and open
Start Market Desk.command. Existing private data stays in ~/.marketdesk-reborn.
Keep a backup of that folder; research JSON exports exclude price histories,
headline snapshots, imports, alerts and AI source data. Do not remove keys.
