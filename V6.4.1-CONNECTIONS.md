V6.4.1 INTELLIGENCE CONNECTIONS
14 November 2026. Answers are local calculations. No language model is required.

THE PANEL
Ask Intelligence opens beside the current page on a wide window and as a full-height sheet on a narrow one. Close keeps the answer. Reopening shows it again. Resize is the blue handle, or the left and right arrow keys while that handle is focused. Cancel stops an older reply from replacing the screen. Retry asks the same question again. A changed page is named before the next question uses it. Use this page switches the next question to the page you are looking at. Attach adds a company symbol and does not change the answer until you ask.

CHART
The return is the change between the two displayed prices. Open the cited chart shows that company and period when the saved prices cover it.

CRYPTO
A saved crypto forecast can be explained. Asking does not write a new report.

HEATMAP, COMPARISON, EVENTS AND FINANCIALS
The button carries the selected company. A chart period is included for the heatmap, comparison and events. A missing filing or announcement is named.

FORECAST
“Has this forecast model beaten its baseline?” reads the check and does not save a new experiment. A learned status is shown only when the model error is smaller than an unchanged price. A later-only improvement is not called a win.

ALERTS
A question does not arm an alert.

SCAN
“Why did this company match my scan?” uses the saved scan. With no saved scan, the answer says to choose one.

PORTFOLIO
“Which holding contributed most?” uses a saved stress test. Mixed currencies are not ranked. With no saved test, the answer says so.

SCENARIO
Growth can be compared with a saved filing. An operating-margin assumption is not subtracted from net margin.

STRATEGY, JOURNAL AND REPORTS
The button carries that area. A research entry opens from the answer when it is in the list. A missing investigation is named. The original investigation is kept when a later version exists.
