MARKET DESK REBORN 2.9 — UNDERSTAND THE BUSINESS

OPEN THE UPDATE
Export your research backup from the old version first. Stop its Terminal
window with Control+C. Unzip this release and double-click Start Market
Desk.command. Keep Terminal open. Your private saved data is reused.

FINANCIAL HEALTH
All tools > Company Overview. Choose a company, then Update SEC accounts.
This uses free official SEC data, without a Finnhub or Massive key. It makes
only a few paced requests, then saves selected facts locally. Saved responses
are reused for 24 hours; their original retrieval dates stay visible.

Read the short explanation and the sales, profit and cash-flow charts.
Choose full financial years or directly reported quarters. Check every figure
opens exact amounts, reporting dates, filing links and accounting definitions.
Download accounts saves a separate JSON copy. Provider profiles and ratios
remain in the expandable section below the new financial health panel.

COMPARE COMPANIES
All tools > Compare Companies. Choose two or three different companies, then
Compare saved accounts. Update selected SEC accounts requests any missing or
old accounts. It does not use Finnhub data limits. The app checks fiscal dates:
if it finds a shared full-year window, it shows that window. Otherwise it shows
latest saved periods with a clear warning and no like-for-like ranking.
Companies with different industries or definitions still need careful reading.
The older provider ratios and provider-comparison report remain available.

INTELLIGENCE
After updating a company's accounts, open Intelligence and select that stock.
Check saved data, then choose Financial health under Quick data questions.
These answers are factual summaries and work without a language model.
The optional installed local AI also receives normalized SEC facts and filing
links. Ask which period it is using. AI drafts still need your review; checks
can reject them, and a valid citation does not establish the interpretation.
Updating accounts does not train either the language model or price model.

WHAT MAY BE MISSING
This first release supports selected consolidated US-GAAP facts in USD from
10-K and 10-Q filers. Foreign IFRS reporters, custom tags and some statement
lines remain unavailable. A missing figure is never assumed to be zero.
Debt means current plus non-current long-term debt only, with both portions
in the same filing. It is not total debt. Other borrowings and leases are not
silently added. Take-Two can show missing long-term debt when a required
standard concept is absent, even though other financial lines are available.
No health score or buy/sell rating is assigned.

SAVED DATA AND PRIVACY
Only selected facts are retained, up to 60 issuers. No new model is downloaded.
The ordinary research backup does not contain the SEC cache; use Download
accounts for its separate JSON copy. Provider keys never go to the SEC.
Opening a view does not refresh SEC data. Nothing runs when the server is off.
If SEC access fails, cached figures remain dated and missing figures remain
missing. There is no proxy or access-restriction bypass.
