MARKET DESK REBORN 4.2.0 — COMPANY EVENTS

1. Stop an older Terminal window with Control+C.
2. Unzip this folder and double-click Start Market Desk.command.
3. Open http://127.0.0.1:8765 and go to Company Overview.
4. Choose TTWO. The Events section reads records already saved on this Mac.
5. Press Update events to check the free news and earnings sources. Filings appear after Update SEC accounts.
6. Select an event. Switch between 1 session and 5 sessions. The dates, closes and SPY comparison stay under the event.
7. Press Show this window on the chart to open the existing price chart for those dates.
8. If no saved item mentions GTA VI, the timeline says so. It does not invent an announcement.

Saved events and comparisons reopen without internet. Source links open only when a connection is available. Keys and personal data stay in ~/.marketdesk-reborn and are not in this ZIP.
