MARKET DESK — SHORT FIRST-USER REVIEW (NOT YET COMPLETED)

Ask someone unfamiliar with the app to try these five tasks. Use public market
data and your own app setup. Do not ask them to enter personal or financial data.
Avoid explaining the interface first; observe where they hesitate.

1. Choose Take-Two and explain in their own words what the company does.
2. Find a stock's one-year chart and explain which dates it actually shows.
3. Choose five years and identify whether some history is missing or a file is
   needed. Explain what the rise/fall percentage compares.
4. Find a headline, its publication date and its original source link. Ask whether
   the app has proved that headline caused the price movement.
5. Locate the detailed workspace, then return to the daily briefing.
