MARKET DESK 3.8 — QUICK GUIDE

OPEN ON YOUR MAC
1. Stop the old version with Control+C in its Terminal window.
2. Unzip MarketDesk-Reborn-v3.8.0-Mac.zip.
3. Open Start Market Desk.command in the extracted folder.
4. Keep Terminal open. If needed, visit http://127.0.0.1:8765.
Keep ~/.marketdesk-reborn to carry forward your saved research, connections and
prices. Python 3 is required. Use the normal macOS approval flow if prompted.

BITCOIN AND ETHEREUM
Open All tools → Crypto. Pick Bitcoin or Ethereum, then a chart period.
Both are USD spot cryptocurrencies. Prices come from Coinbase Exchange.
The public feed works with an internet connection and requires no account.
The blue line shows completed candle closes. The green line shows recent
streamed trades. The large price shows the exchange's most recent available
trade, with its source and time. When the stream is quiet or disconnected,
the app checks timestamped REST snapshots. Previously loaded chart ranges
and snapshots can be read from the local cache while offline.
Period change compares the first and last displayed candle closes.
Blank intervals show missing observations. All chart times are in UTC.
Crypto has its own workspace and its own cache for the two USD pairs.

RESTORE A PAST EQUITY CHART
1. Open a US company in Overview.
2. Choose 1hr for the final recorded hour or 24hrs for the full provider date.
3. Expand Understand this chart and click Load last available US date.
The date is the latest eligible completed New York trading date from Massive.
Any extended-hours bars on that date are included. Holidays and weekends
use the supported equity calendar. The Massive Basic feed serves past New
York dates after midnight ET. The restore uses your existing Massive key
and shared request allowance. A previously saved date can be opened offline.
Use Return to recorded prices or pick a chart range to switch back.
Provider minutes stay separate from the current recorded stream and alerts.

STORAGE
The ZIP contains the app and guides. Your private data remains in the existing
local database. The added caches hold two crypto pairs across five chart
ranges and their latest snapshots, plus up to 30 saved equity minute series.
