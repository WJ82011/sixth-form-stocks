MARKET DESK V3.4 — ANALYST LAB

OPEN THE UPDATE
1. Export a full research backup from Settings in your old version.
2. Stop its Terminal window with Control+C.
3. Extract MarketDesk-Reborn-v3.4.0-Mac.zip.
4. Open the extracted folder and double-click Start Market Desk.command.
5. Keep Terminal open and visit http://127.0.0.1:8765.
   If Terminal prints another address, use it. Close the older app to keep
   using port 8765 and your existing browser workspace.

If the launcher does not run: type python3 and a space in Terminal, drag the
new server.py into Terminal and press Return. Install Python 3 from python.org
if it is missing. No Node.js installation is needed to use Market Desk.
Keep your existing ~/.marketdesk-reborn folder. Do not delete your keys,
recorded prices, local model or saved research.

FIND THE NEW TOOLS
All tools → Market Desk Intelligence → Analyst Lab at the top.

1. Enter a symbol, such as TTWO, and load saved accounts. If none are saved,
   press Update SEC accounts. Some companies lack supported statement facts.
   Check the annual dates, accounting basis, currency and source filing.
2. Open Source desk for saved headlines and economic indicators. These are
   context to investigate, not automatic causes or trained news signals.
3. Decide how much existing revenue is affected and how costs behave.
   Variable costs move with sales volume; the remaining operating costs are
   treated as fixed in this simplified worksheet. Explain the classification.
4. Start unchanged-business references, then edit Downside, Base and Upside.
   This button only sets zero changes and no launch. It is not a forecast.
5. Add source records or clearly labelled assumptions. Keep what a report
   actually says separate from what you think it might mean.
6. Optional launch: supply company net receipts, annualized run-rate units,
   selling month, recognised-revenue share, cannibalisation and cost share.
   Attach an evidence record and avoid counting the launch twice.
7. Compare cases. Valuation multiples and share/ADR calculations are optional
   and require explicit inputs. Missing inputs stay unavailable.
8. Save the analysis. It becomes a dated, immutable record included in your
   full research backup. Review, download or archive it under Saved analyses.
   Up to 20 records and 30 evidence items per record are retained. Archive
   before permanently removing a record; download it first if wanted.

Draft forms are not saved automatically. Save a complete analysis before
closing the tab; an unsaved-change warning helps prevent accidental loss.
Changing assumptions invalidates earlier calculated results until recalculated.
Source inputs changing on the server require a reload and a fresh review.

TRAIN THE PRICE MODEL
Open Separate experiment and press Train and test saved price data. It needs
at least 220 verified saved provider daily closes. Load daily history through
the existing company chart tools first. Imported CSV prices are not silently
substituted into this experiment. The current New York session is excluded.

It forecasts five observed closes, not a guaranteed calendar week. It reports
historical test errors, an unchanged-price baseline, learned factor weights,
prior-error range coverage and missing/stale data. Negative skill means it
performed worse than predicting no price change. A short successful test is
not proof that it will work in future. Old data withholds the current estimate.

ASK THE LOCAL AI
Save an analysis, then choose Ask Intelligence about this company. Press the
existing Ask Market Desk AI button yourself. The assistant receives the latest
active saved worksheet for that company as conditional evidence. It can explain
the assumptions and calculations; it is not being retrained by them. Existing
source checks still apply. A local model must already be installed for local
AI answers. No paid OpenAI service or new model download is required by V3.4.

For Take-Two, TTWO is the listed company; Rockstar is a subsidiary. A game's
retail price is not automatically company net receipts. Net bookings and GAAP
recognised revenue are different measures. No GTA release date, sales volume
or future price has been invented for you.

Nothing records or learns while this server or your Mac is off. Read
V3.4-METHODOLOGY.md for formulas, source links and practical limits.
