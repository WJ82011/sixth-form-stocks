MARKET DESK AI — SIMPLE GUIDE / v2.3.0

1. Stop the older Market Desk Terminal window with Control+C.
2. Open Ollama. This Mac already has qwen3.5:4b installed.
3. Unzip this update and open Start Market Desk.command. Keep Terminal open.
4. Open Market Desk Intelligence. Choose a stock and press Check saved data.
5. Check that Local AI ready appears. Ask a question or write a research report.
6. Open the supporting excerpts and original references before using a draft.

TO GIVE IT BETTER SOURCES
Press Refresh company sources for company figures and news through Finnhub.
Your existing free-plan budgets still apply. Missing or restricted data stays
missing. Asking the AI does not automatically request new market data.
Paste an excerpt from an annual report or earnings release in Add a company
report excerpt. Add a title, date and original link, then save it for the stock.
You can also load .txt or .md files. PDF parsing and web browsing are not included.
Original links are references only: the app does not fetch them.

COMPARE AND WRITE
Choose another watched stock in Optional company comparison. Refresh company
sources if needed, then ask about the differences. Check currencies and periods.
Tick Use the previous draft for a follow-up. Previous AI text is conversation
context, not independent evidence. Current source evidence still decides facts.
Use Open in report editor to review, edit, save or print a research draft.
Verification boxes remain unchecked. The stock must be on your watchlist.
Download draft creates a readable Markdown copy with references.

IF AI IS UNAVAILABLE
Open Ollama and press Check AI setup. The local service must be running at
http://127.0.0.1:11434 and have a local text model installed. No cloud model is
used. The model is separate from this small ZIP. No model is auto-downloaded.
On another Mac, install Ollama from https://ollama.com/download and use its
model library to install a suitable local model. Larger models need more RAM.
The checked model on this 16 GB Apple Silicon Mac was qwen3.5:4b (4 GB weights).
Model responses can take a minute or longer. A draft can have one automatic
correction attempt if its source references fail; checks are never relaxed.
If no draft passes, ask a narrower question or improve the source excerpts.
Do not start several drafts at once. Closing the browser does not cancel a
running job while the server stays open. Check AI setup offers resume; accepted
drafts remain in Saved AI drafts for that stock. Server shutdown stops jobs.
Quick data questions, charts and the statistical model work without Ollama.

YOUR DATA
Sources and accepted drafts stay in the private ~/.marketdesk-reborn database.
These are excluded from the research JSON backup. Keep that private folder
when upgrading. Download important drafts separately. Saved drafts keep the
original copied source snippets even after you remove the original excerpt.
Delete a saved draft separately to remove its snapshot from the app.
Up to 20 source documents per stock, 100 in total, and 100 accepted AI drafts
in total are retained. The library shows the most recent 30 drafts per stock.
The model does not retrain itself or learn while your Mac is off.
AI drafts can be wrong even when references match. Review the source text.
