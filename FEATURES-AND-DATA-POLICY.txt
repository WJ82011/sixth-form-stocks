MARKET DESK v1.6.2 — FEATURES & MARKET-DATA TRUST POLICY
========================================================

SOURCE PRIORITY AND HONEST LABELS
--------------------------------
1. FINNHUB WEBSOCKET: A verified, positive-volume, timestamped trade event
   may be labelled a verified live trade if connection/time checks pass.
   This is NOT necessarily consolidated whole-market or SIP coverage.
2. FINNHUB REST: Genuine stock quote snapshot. A >15-minute provider age
   is labelled STALE QUOTE. A connected socket is NOT proof of live ticks.
3. MASSIVE US DAILY OHLC: For D resolution when configured, Stocks Basic
   supplies split-adjusted end-of-day bars from Massive REST. Each bar is
   checked for ticker, price-range consistency, volume, timestamp and order.
   These are NOT live trades; missing weekends/holidays remain empty.
4. GENUINE LOCAL CACHE: Only bars already validated from Massive are stored
   in private SQLite across restarts. When a refresh fails and prior bars
   are adequately complete, the UI explicitly discloses stored-data use.
5. FINNHUB DAILY OHLC: If Massive is not configured, try real Finnhub
   historical candles where the account permits; restricted endpoints
   display a clear error rather than fabricated history.
6. LOCALLY RECORDED PRICE POINTS: Observations from Finnhub, not a full
   market-wide historical feed. Sparse time ranges remain incomplete.
7. PURPLE FORECAST: Research-model scenario derived from >=45 genuine
   recent daily closes spanning >=55 days; no daily history means NO line.
   Five-, 20- and 60-session trends, diminishing momentum and observed
   volatility drive a curved research scenario. Headlines are contextual
   only, not numerical prediction inputs. Model-implied bands are NOT
   calibrated. Historical 5/20-session checks compare the model with an
   unchanged-price baseline and do NOT establish future predictive skill.

PROVIDER SEPARATION
-------------------
Finnhub stays responsible for quote snapshots, trade streaming, company
information, ticker search, and headlines. Massive is used exclusively for
US daily historical OHLC bars and derived research scenarios, not for live
WebSockets. Free Massive Basic limits are respected with a bounded 4/min
local request budget and 6-hour/disk caches of previously downloaded bars.

WORKSPACES PRESERVED
--------------------
Overview; Market Terminal (up to 60 watched stocks); Live Tape with Time &
Sales and source indicators; Company Intelligence; Forecast Lab; Market
News; Research Notes, export and backup; Settings & Diagnostics; Professional
Terminal and Signal Edition; accessibility/appearance preferences.

LOCAL DATA
----------
~/.marketdesk-reborn/finnhub_key          private Finnhub key (0600)
~/.marketdesk-reborn/massive_key          private Massive key (0600)
~/.marketdesk-reborn/observations.sqlite3 local observations, captured trades,
                                          validated Massive daily bar cache
~/.marketdesk-reborn/workspace.json       watchlist, notes, preferences
Browser localStorage                       editable workspace cache

SECURITY
--------
Only localhost is served; HTTPS verification is enforced for both providers.
Massive credentials travel solely in an HTTPS Authorization Bearer header,
never query parameters, and are not sent back to the browser. Finnhub token
must remain in the local browser for direct Finnhub streaming, as already
implemented. Neither private key is included in research backup exports.
Do not deploy the existing localhost app as a public website without a new
multi-user security model and server-side stream relay.

NOT CLAIMED
-----------
No always-live global market prices, consolidated whole-market tape, Level II
order book, trading/execution capability, full international market history,
accurate future stock predictions, guaranteed intervals or investment advice.

V1.6.2 MASSIVE ENTITLEMENT POLICY
--------------------------------
Basic excludes the current New York date, even after market close; requests
clip to the previous NY date and within a conservative 2-year historical
window. A documented DELAYED status with validated bars is valid historical
data. Neither DELAYED nor EOD is presented as a live quote.
Provider error text is bounded and private API keys are removed before display.

CHART CONTINUITY AND MARKET DATA INTEGRITY — v1.6.4
------------------------------------------------
* 1D is a local recording, not a continuous exchange feed. When the Mac is
  off, missing time is not backfilled or drawn as observed prices.
* 1W/1M/3M/1Y use authenticated verified daily EOD bars when available;
  they do not splice irregular local ticks into a daily close sequence.
* A purple dashed forecast needs adequate, recent, genuine daily closes.
  Selecting the overlay while on 1D moves to 1Y; failures remain explicit.

V1.6.4 ADAPTIVE RESEARCH FORECAST / LIMITATIONS
---------------------------------------------
* Derived entirely from verified provider daily closing prices and past
  return statistics. No synthetic price history or invented future candles.
* The centre curve fades shorter-horizon momentum rapidly while longer-term
  trends decay more slowly. A flat centre is legitimate when the data are flat.
* Recent volatility shrinks speculative trend extrapolation; illustrative
  low/high fan widens with horizon and has no promised coverage probability.
* Historic backtests with non-overlapping 5- and 20-session outcomes show
  model MAPE next to the naive unchanged-price baseline. A poor result is
  shown explicitly rather than hidden. Backtesting itself does not prove
  predictive ability, particularly with only two years of end-of-day data.
* Finnhub headline keywords are a contextual signal only; not used to
  calculate target prices and never presented as a trained AI predictor.
* The forecast is not an investment recommendation, trading signal or
  realistic realised future stock-price path. It may be completely wrong.
