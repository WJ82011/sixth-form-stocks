MARKET DESK REBORN v2.4.0 — ROUNDED BLACK & BLUE

An original visual redesign of v2.3.0: dark blue surfaces, soft rounded cards,
blue primary controls, clearer system typography, quieter dividers and more
space around chart controls. Both Signal and Professional editions are updated.

CHARTS
Overview now puts the main chart ahead of the market-pulse cards. Professional
quote cards form one horizontal strip so the chart appears sooner on screen.
Larger high-resolution canvases, clearer axis labels, lighter vertical guides,
and a two-axis crosshair on Overview. Line joins remain rounded, but observed
prices are not smoothed or interpolated. Recorded gaps remain gaps; isolated
points remain dots. A new last-plotted-observation label includes its original
time and explicitly avoids a live-price guarantee. Full-screen charts keep
rounded corners. Custom chart sizes still work. Downward series remain red;
blue upward lines, green/red changes and purple forecast estimates are distinct.

PRESERVED
All 17 workspaces, the local AI model interface, sources and drafts, price
alerts, the 166-stock directory, Take-Two/Rockstar search, company comparisons,
reports, notes, paper portfolio, indicators, valuation, model results, provider
limits and private-data storage. No paid chart library or new provider plan.
Live updates still depend on genuine Finnhub responses and your connection.
This design does not expand market data coverage or monitor while the Mac is off.

YOUR PREFERENCES
On first launch, the old default Brass accent becomes Blue. Edition, layout,
chart size, contrast, ticker and motion choices stay saved. Brass, Cyan, Mint
and Violet are still available in Settings; choosing Brass afterwards persists.
The Blue choice is included in research preference backups. Reduced-motion
and high-contrast controls remain available. Small screens use a menu drawer
and stacked panels; wide tables scroll within their panels.

UPGRADE
Stop the old Terminal window with Control+C, unzip this update, and open
Start Market Desk.command. Keep the private ~/.marketdesk-reborn folder.
Open Ollama to use local AI. The model does not need to be downloaded again.
No keys, user database, test price records or model weights are in the ZIP.
Read STORAGE-GUIDE.txt, DESIGN-ORIGINALITY.txt and V2.4-QA-REPORT.txt.
