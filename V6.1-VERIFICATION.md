WHAT THE FORECAST DOES
The purple line is not drawn from a decorative curve. For each of 1, 5 and 20 sessions, a price model is compared with an unchanged price on an earlier block. Only if it wins there is it checked again on a later block. The line, when it is allowed, is fit without those later outcomes. The band is the middle range of the earlier misses. The later dates then say how often the actual close fell inside that band. A recent-average move is also scored. It does not override the unchanged-price rule. News and accounts are not inputs. Each distinct result is saved. Issued forecasts are not replaced.
