MARKET DESK REBORN v2.6.1 — LONGER PRICE HISTORY

Adds 6M, 2Y, 5Y and 10Y alongside existing 1Y and shorter chart ranges.
Long-range requests are sized for the chosen range, cached by ticker/depth and
kept within the existing free provider restrictions. A larger range does not
claim access to older data. Earlier missing years remain empty on the chart.

The chart reports its actual dates and percentage rise/fall between displayed
prices. Longer axes include the year. Partial and imported coverage are visible
without opening the explanation panel. Daily gaps are not bridged.

Adds an optional local daily CSV import for longer histories without requiring
a paid API. Imports keep source and adjustment labels; they do not alter live
quotes, recorded intraday prices, provider models or AI training. Forecasts are
withheld for imported chart data. Existing forecast and indicator workspaces
explicitly continue requesting provider data.

Imports are atomic, validated and stored separately in the existing private
SQLite database. Source names are rendered as text, not executable markup.
POST actions require the existing localhost-origin and local-request checks.

All heatmap, motion, watchlist, company/news and research features retained.
No paid OpenAI dependency or extra local model is added. Original source files
remain available in the prior v2.6.0 folder. Stop the old app before launching
Start Market Desk.command from this newly extracted folder.

Read LONG-TERM-PRICE-GUIDE.txt for simple use, coverage and backup instructions.
Read V2.6.1-QA-REPORT.txt for validation results.
