MARKET DESK 2.1.0 — EXPANDED STOCK DIRECTORY
3 November 2026

166 US-listed stocks across 14 browsing categories, including technology,
chips, games, media, shops, travel, banking, healthcare, energy, industry,
food, utilities, property, cars and materials (some share a category).

Open Stock Browser to browse, filter by category or search a company name.
Use Add to watchlist to track a stock, or Open stock if it is already saved.
The directory works offline and does not fetch prices as you browse. Finnhub
search remains available below it for companies outside the curated directory.
Rockstar/GTA aliases still point to Take-Two Interactive (TTWO).

Existing watchlists stay unchanged; the default list is not expanded to 166.
The 60-stock watchlist cap, quote budget, polling batches and live-feed limit
are unchanged. Only watched stocks are requested through the existing cycle.
A larger watchlist takes longer to refresh. Provider access remains plan-based.

All selected symbols were checked against Nasdaq's listing files at build time.
Test symbols and ETFs were excluded. The dated catalogue includes company names,
exchange, listing currency and categories; it does not bundle exchange prices.
Some overseas companies use US-listed depositary shares. Categories are simple
browsing groups, not official sector classifications. Listings can change.
See STOCK-DIRECTORY-SOURCES.txt for sources and scope.

US-listed directory prices use USD even when a company profile uses a different
home-country reporting currency. Other financial figures keep their source units.
