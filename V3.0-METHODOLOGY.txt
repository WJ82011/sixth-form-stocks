MARKET DESK 3.0 — COVERAGE AND METHODS

STATEMENTS
SEC Company Facts supplies selected standard consolidated concepts, not every
line, extension tag or footnote. US GAAP and IFRS, and their disclosed monetary
currencies, are separate books. A disclosed presentation currency does not
establish an issuer's functional currency. ADR prices, shares and statement
currencies are not automatically converted. Check the filing itself.
Quarter differences use matching concepts, currency and fiscal-year starts,
with ordered filings and valid durations. TTM requires four contiguous
quarters. Derived inputs are exposed. Historical facts use latest available
filings and revisions; they are unsuitable for point-in-time model backtests.
Manual/custom lines remain distinct and do not silently replace SEC facts.
The original V2 financial-health panel retains its narrower US-GAAP/USD scope.
The valuation source worksheet shows an operating cash minus PPE-purchases
proxy only when windows/filings match. This is not unlevered free cash flow,
a valuation recommendation or an automatic DCF/share-count/ADR conversion.

PRACTICE ACCOUNTING
USD base; Decimal FIFO lots; fees enter cost or sale proceeds as appropriate.
Dividend deductions are explicit, deposits/withdrawals are external flows,
splits change quantity without creating profit. Standalone fees reduce cash.
Non-USD values require dated explicit USD-per-unit FX and a source. No implied
parity or automatic historical FX. All entries are hypothetical, not orders.
Manual zero prices are possible for explicitly sourced write-offs; missing
marks are not zero. Marks older than seven calendar days withhold the current
complete total. Checkpoints require same-date prices and FX for every holding,
store captured inputs, and are checked against the ledger fingerprint.
Modified Dietz estimates a return using end-of-day weighted external cash
flows between valid checkpoints. This is an approximation, not exact daily
time-weighted performance. Benchmark prices must exist on both endpoint dates.
The benchmark is a price return excluding dividends, fees and FX; it is not a
total-return or risk-matched outperformance claim. FIFO is not a tax report.

EVENTS / DISCOVERY / MACRO
SEC recent filing history is completed activity, not predicted earnings dates.
Finnhub earnings access may be restricted. All provider dates are estimated;
no exact release time is invented. Manual events have explicit IANA zones,
status, sources and revision history. Ambiguous DST times must be clarified.
The official SEC directory broadens listing search; saved-account screening
is limited to loaded, comparable standardized annual accounts. It does not
scan all listed stocks or guarantee third-party provider access.
Cap-map areas use positive USD company profiles retrieved within 30 days;
profile retrieval is not an exact valuation timestamp. Colour uses sufficiently
recent saved quotes. Coverage is a subset, not an index or free-float weighting.
Comparison rebases saved Massive closes to 100 on shared dates, without FX,
fees or dividend adjustments; history/access gaps are stated, never filled.
FRED observations are monthly/quarterly, may be revised, and are not release
timestamps. CPI/GDP year changes require an actual matching year-ago period.

AI AND DATA COST
Existing local statistical training and chronological out-of-sample evaluation
remain. Forecast coverage and uncertainty remain visible. Local Ollama answers
can use expanded dated SEC evidence alongside existing company/chart/news
packets. Numeric/citation checks can reject an answer; they do not prove every
sentence correct. Treat model output as fallible research and inspect sources.
No paid OpenAI is required. No extra model is downloaded. Training, updates,
alerts and collection do not run while the Mac/app is off. No automatic new
background scanning of thousands of symbols. Existing free-provider pacing,
caches, plan restrictions and SEC request spacing remain in effect.
