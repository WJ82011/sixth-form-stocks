MARKET DESK INTELLIGENCE 2.0 — METHODS

Historical overview
Uses only source-labelled local Finnhub observations with finite positive
prices and provider timestamps no later than analysis time. Filters to the
last wall-clock hour or 24 hours; sorts and deduplicates timestamps. Daily
bars and future points are rejected. Reports first/last timestamps, endpoint
percentage change, observed extrema and timestamps, occupied one-minute slots,
internal gaps >5 minutes and staleness >5 minutes. Completeness always false.
No interpolation, annualisation or claim of exchange-wide extrema. Local
retention is at most 5000 points per symbol. Missing window edges are evident
from first/last times; internal gaps do not include missing window edges.
Closed markets are not classified as recording failures: gaps can have many
causes. Intraday charts break connecting lines after gaps over five minutes.

Learning lab
Only stored Massive split-adjusted daily closes are fitted. Finnhub daily
charts remain supported by existing features but are not model training data
in this lab. Up to 500 closes; minimum 90; adjacent bars must be 20 hours to
5 days apart. No future data. One-lag ridge autoregression on daily log returns:
y = intercept + coefficient * previous log return, penalty 0.01 on slope only.
Penalty is fixed before testing; intercept unpenalised. Learned parameters
are recalculated from available history per request and exported as evidence.
There is no persistent optimiser, cloud training or background learning.

Expanding-window tests start with 60 predictor/target pairs. At test i only
pairs before i are fitted. Target is the next observed trading-session log
return. Each target appears once. Model and unchanged-price benchmark errors
are abs(predicted close / actual close - 1), averaged and shown as percentages.
Directional accuracy includes all targets; zero is classified as non-positive.
No fee-adjusted trading return is claimed. Split-adjusted closes are not total
returns and corporate actions/dividends may affect comparability.

Empirical band
First two-thirds of chronological OOS residuals calibrate the 90th percentile
of absolute log errors (minimum 10 calibration points). The remaining errors
measure later held-out coverage; their targets were never used to calibrate
that radius. Subsequent expanding-window fits may use earlier evaluation
returns, as an online forecaster would. Final estimate uses all available
pairs; band applies calibration radius around it in log space. This is an
empirical error band, not a calibrated guarantee or conformal claim. Effective
samples may be small and regime changes can destroy coverage. Stale histories
are flagged; extreme test returns/predictions or invalid spacing withhold output.

Questions and news
A local keyword/topic router selects evidence templates; it is not an LLM or
open-ended company analyst. Supported topics can be combined. It cannot answer
all possible questions and says so. Company/news load only on request via the
existing Finnhub cache, request budget and backoff. Profile/metric retrieval
and article publication times are separate. News text is escaped and never
executed or used as model instructions. Only http/https article links are shown.
No fabricated headlines, causal explanations or fresh quote claims.
