MARKET DESK v2.7 — SIMPLE GUIDE

1. Start the app and open Overview.
2. Daily briefing shows your watchlist's recorded moves and dated headlines.
   A price quote can be old. Read its time, especially outside trading hours.
3. Choose any company card, or choose Company & chart. Read its short business
   summary, then explore the chart. More company details opens the background
   fields; Open full company research retains the detailed financial page.
4. Read the small label under a chart period before selecting it. Saved daily
   data means the dates reach that period's beginning/recent end; gaps can still
   exist. Partial file/data means part of the period is missing. Import needed
   means the current free source cannot provide the full requested years.
5. Manage price history opens source dates, saved imports and the sourcing guide.
   Add or replace opens the original daily-price CSV form for the selected ticker.
   Download keeps a copy of that ticker's imported daily prices. Keep the original
   source notes too. Use provider history removes the app's imported copy for that
   stock, leaving your original CSV and saved provider bars intact.
6. Read the headlines' publication and retrieval dates. Company headlines are
   matched by company-name or ticker text in the title; this can miss relevant
   articles and is not a verification of the article's claims.
7. Detailed workspace returns to the earlier Signal or Professional layout.
   Your edition preference and all sidebar tools remain available in Settings.

Refresh up to 8 prices & headlines requests at most eight watchlist quotes and
one general-news list. Repeated refreshes prioritise the oldest checked stocks.
Update this company & news requests the selected quote, profile and news list.
A request may reuse the provider cache. Neither button guarantees a live trade.
Opening the briefing only rereads saved snapshots; the existing app-wide price
cycle continues. No updates or model training happen while the Mac is off.

WHAT YOU NEED
Existing Finnhub connection for new quotes and headlines; Massive connection
for new provider daily prices, subject to its free window. Saved Massive daily
bars can be viewed offline. A suitable imported daily CSV can provide older
history without a paid API. See LONG-TERM-PRICE-GUIDE.txt for file requirements.

BACKUP
Research JSON exports do not contain prices, imports, saved headlines, alerts,
AI drafts or sources. Keep original CSVs. For a full local-data backup, stop the
app, then copy ~/.marketdesk-reborn privately. Keep provider keys private.

Company descriptions are general directory background, not automated analysis
of why a price changed. A provider industry field is clearly dated when used.
No AI-generated scores or invented facts are added to the daily briefing.
