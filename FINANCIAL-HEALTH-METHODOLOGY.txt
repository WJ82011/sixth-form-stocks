IDENTITY AND ACCESS
Resolve a ticker through the cached official company_tickers.json directory.
Four bootstrap candidates (AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, TTWO) can avoid a directory fetch,
but they are not accepted on that basis: each refresh verifies the requested
ticker against the current SEC submissions issuer record and checks the
companyfacts CIK. Dot/dash share-class notation is normalized. Same-issuer
listings cannot be treated as independent companies in SEC comparison.

Requests use an identified MarketDeskReborn educational research User-Agent,
fixed HTTPS SEC endpoints, existing verified macOS CA roots and no redirects.
No key or personal research is transmitted. An advanced user may set their
own declared application/contact header with MARKETDESK_SEC_USER_AGENT;
if supplied, that header goes only to SEC requests. No contact address is
invented. SEC may restrict access independently of these controls.

One refresh runs at a time, at most about one request per second. No automatic
SEC polling, no retries and no alternate-host bypass. HTTP 403/429 starts a
five-minute shared cooldown. Issuer results are reused for 24 hours, ticker
mapping for seven days. A response is limited to 20 MiB. Only normalized
selected facts are stored, not whole multi-megabyte raw Company Facts files.

SUPPORTED FACTS
Only standard us-gaap consolidated facts in USD, from 10-K/10-K/A and
10-Q/10-Q/A. Annual durations are 330–400 inclusive days and must come from a
10-K variant. Direct quarters are 75–105 days. This excludes nine-month and
six-month year-to-date cash flow. Quarter four is not synthesized, and no
trailing-twelve-month aggregate is inferred. Instant facts must match the
reporting period's exact end date. Future filings/dates, invalid accessions,
non-finite values and boolean amounts are rejected. Filing FY labels are not
used to assign the fact's year: actual start/end dates determine the window.

Revenue concepts are prioritized: RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcluding-
AssessedTax, Revenues, SalesRevenueNet. Goods-only or service-only figures
are not substituted for total sales. Other concepts are explicit in the UI:
NetIncomeLoss, NetCashProvidedByUsedInOperatingActivities, CashAndCashEquiv-
alentsAtCarryingValue, Assets, Liabilities, LongTermDebtCurrent and
LongTermDebtNoncurrent. Restricted cash and lease-inclusive debt are not
substituted. No absent debt portion is inferred to be zero.

VERSION AND CALCULATION RULES
For each exact fact window use the latest filed eligible concept, with
concept preference breaking ties. Ambiguous same-concept/same-filing values
are withheld. Amendments and differing values across filings are disclosed;
differing values alone are not called a proven restatement. Historical
series use latest-filed comparative versions, not a point-in-time dataset.

Net margin needs positive revenue and profit from the same window and
accession. Long-term debt needs both portions from the same filing/end date.
Annual revenue growth needs the same revenue concept and USD unit, a positive
base, consecutive periods and comparable durations (within 14 days). Missing
periods and concept changes withhold growth. All displayed percentages are
explicit calculations; they are not reported quotes or investment forecasts.

COMPARISON
Use the most recent common annual window, allowing start/end dates within
seven days and durations within 14 days for ordinary fiscal calendar shifts.
Actual dates remain visible. Without a common window, label the latest annual
figures as unmatched and do not rank or calculate like-for-like differences.
Matching windows do not resolve all accounting-definition or industry issues.
No FX conversion or financial health score is invented.

INTELLIGENCE AND LIMITATIONS
Quick financial questions use deterministic summaries of the selected saved
facts. Optional local AI uses normalized fact records grouped by source
filing, with precise periods, units and original filing-index links. Exact
million-USD conversions are labelled as Market Desk's normalized presentation,
not verbatim quotations from filings. Citation and numeric checks remain
unchanged. A targeted SEC consistency check also rejects known false claims
that supplied filing dates or prior-year records are missing. This is not
full semantic verification; a person must review each AI draft.
Neither accounts nor an AI summary enter the existing price-model training
or backtests. Existing price-only model uncertainty and limits remain.

This is a deliberately bounded first financial-statements feature. Custom
XBRL extensions, IFRS, complete financial statements, segment reporting,
lease normalization, TTM construction and point-in-time backtests remain
future work. Do not treat missing coverage as an absence of business risk.
