MARKET DESK v2.6.1 — STOCK PRICES OVER THE YEARS

1. Open Overview and choose a stock in your watchlist.
2. Choose 1Y, 2Y, 5Y or 10Y above its chart. 6M is also available.
3. Read the dates and the rise/fall summary below the chart. It compares the
   first and last displayed prices, not a made-up full-period return.

WHAT LOADS AUTOMATICALLY
Massive daily history uses your existing key. The app requests enough dates
for the selected period, but conservatively keeps Massive within its free
Stocks Basic window: about two years, ending before the current New York date.
The 1Y option already existed; its loader previously requested only 430 days.
This update supports longer requests, preserves longer imported series and
labels partial history visibly. Paid Massive plans are not required and are
not auto-detected. Finnhub daily access still depends on your plan.

A full 5- or 10-year series is NOT supplied automatically by Massive Basic.
Choosing 5Y on a free two-year series shows the two years at their real dates,
leaves earlier time empty and says Partial 5Y history. No prices are generated.

HOW TO ADD FIVE OR TEN YEARS WITHOUT A PAID API
Get a daily-price CSV export for your selected stock from a source you trust
and are allowed to use. Under its chart, open Longer history · import daily
prices. Select the file, name the source, choose its split adjustment and
confirm the ticker is correct. Click Import for selected stock.

Required columns: Date and Close, or Date and Adj Close / Adjusted Close.
Dates must be YYYY-MM-DD, from 2000 through today. Extra OHLC/volume columns
are ignored. An optional Symbol column must match the selected stock.
One row per date; at least 2, at most 10,000 rows; file smaller than 900 KB.
Prices must be positive finite numbers. Invalid files leave the old import
untouched. Rows are sorted. Adjusted Close takes priority over Close.
Check the source: an adjusted-close series may adjust for both splits and
other corporate actions. The app cannot verify its methodology. Unadjusted
prices can show stock splits as abrupt price falls or rises.

The import is saved for that ticker only. It supplies the daily chart until
you choose Use provider history. That button removes this app's imported copy
for the selected ticker; it does not delete your original CSV or provider data.
No CSV files or example market prices are included in the release ZIP.

WHAT THE SUMMARY MEANS
Percentage change = (last displayed price / first displayed price - 1) x 100.
Actual endpoint dates and prices appear alongside it. This is a change in the
selected price series, not independently calculated total investment return.
No separate dividends, fees or currency conversions are added. An imported
adjusted-close file may already reflect its source's dividend adjustments.
Missing years are not silently treated as flat prices. Daily gaps longer than
10 days break the line. Axis labels include years for long ranges. A partial
history label appears if the beginning is more than 14 days late or the last
record is more than 10 days old; exact dates always remain visible.

Imported files are user-supplied, not independently verified. They are not
mixed into provider daily bars or intraday observations. Forecast overlays,
Price Forecasts, Indicator Studio and the server's AI/statistical training
continue to use provider data; imported files do not train or validate them.
The 1-hour and 24-hour charts retain their observed timestamped prices only.

BACKUP AND STORAGE
Imports stay on this Mac in the existing private observations.sqlite3 database
inside ~/.marketdesk-reborn. Research JSON exports do not include price data.
Keep your original CSVs and back up the private folder while the app is stopped.
No new model download or chart library is required.
