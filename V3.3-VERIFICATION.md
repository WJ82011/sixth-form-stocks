# V3.3 saved-source briefing — verification

V3.3 adds the Home **What changed?** briefing while retaining all V3.2 projects,
named lists, notes, reports, chart tools, financial research and V3.1 recovery.
The comparison is explicitly **since the last saved review point**, not an
inferred complete market history since a visit. Review points change only on
an explicit action and are scoped to the actual company set.

## Browser walkthrough

The final clean preview opened all **22 destinations at desktop 1280 × 900
and phone 390 × 844**: 44 checks, no missing destination and no whole-page
horizontal overflow. All five briefing tabs also fitted phone width. Dark and
light briefing screenshots were inspected. No browser console errors were
observed in the final sweep. This is a Chromium in-app browser with simulated
phone dimensions, not a claim about every physical device or browser.

An isolated preview used genuine saved public data with no provider credentials:

- First visit showed source coverage and no invented new-item or price-change
  claim. Setting a point and re-reading the same records produced zero newly
  comparable quotes/headlines/filings.

  a later genuine saved Finnhub quote was copied into the disposable database.
  The briefing showed the two original timestamps and -1.53% endpoint change,
  with the older-than-review nature of the newly loaded quote disclosed.
- Newly loaded real saved headline records retained publication/retrieval dates;
  earlier articles were labelled as such, including provider feed-scope caveats.
- A single-company named list received its own review point. Returning to the
  original sixteen-company list restored the earlier comparison unchanged.
- A clearly labelled test-only manual calendar event appeared as date-only and
  estimated. After review, changing its date/status to cancelled produced a
  revision and removed it from Upcoming. It was never presented as an actual
  company announcement.
- Keyboard arrow navigation selected the next briefing tab. Coverage displayed

A preview-export automation attempt stalled because it closed an already open

Original user credentials, projects and market database were not modified by

## Coverage and limits

This is a deterministic local saved-record comparison, not new AI training,
a real-time news service, a whole-market scan, or a causal market explanation.
Raw quote comparisons are not adjusted for corporate actions, dividends, fees
or FX. Review points are portable user workspace records, not an independent
attestation that imported history or user edits are authentic market data.

Provider-scoped headlines may be irrelevant. Source links are displayed with
publisher attribution rather than article bodies. Filing dates without times
cannot establish intraday ordering. Manual event confirmation remains user
sourced; provider earnings remain estimates. Missing data never proves inactivity.

Reading the briefing makes no external request. Explicit one-company refreshes
reuse existing shared budgets/caches; the ordinary app's existing quote loops
are unchanged. Market data still needs provider connectivity, and nothing runs
while the Mac/app is off. There is no paid AI requirement or new model download.

Up to eight company-group points are kept, bounded to 512 KB. Each comparison
covers up to 60 companies, 200 headline IDs, 200 filing IDs and 80 nearby events.
Capped/old/missing records remain coverage limitations; this is not a perpetual
unread inbox or complete historical archive. Full workspace backups include
these endpoints and references, while raw price databases and keys remain
separate. Earlier release guides/results describe their historical baselines.
