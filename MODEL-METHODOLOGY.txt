MARKET DESK REBORN v1.6.4 — RESEARCH FORECAST METHODOLOGY
===========================================================

PURPOSE
-------
The violet dashed path is an experimental scenario to make statistical
assumptions visible. It is NOT a promise of future prices, trading advice,
or a claim to outperform a naive forecast. A curve alone does not establish
predictive accuracy; markets are noisy and surprise events matter.

DATA SOURCE AND ELIGIBILITY
---------------------------
Only genuine, provider-verified historical DAILY closing prices from Massive
(split-adjusted EOD) or Finnhub's historical daily endpoint are eligible.
Never infer past closing prices from intraday ticks captured on this Mac.
Require 45+ unique daily observations spanning 55+ calendar days and a last
close within ten calendar days. Display the provider identity on the chart.
Store the provider data separately from projected values.

MODEL (DETERMINISTIC, NO RANDOM WAVES)
--------------------------------------
For each session t, compute the observed log-return:
    r[t] = ln(close[t] / close[t-1])
Compute the averages over the most recent 5, 20 and 60 return observations.
Compute exponentially weighted historical volatility using a 0.965 decay,
with a minimum volatility floor. Dampen trend signals when that measured
volatility is high; cap all daily expected-return components.

Three components contribute to the future return forecast:
  1. SLOW:   a shrunk long-run trend from the last 60 returns.
  2. MEDIUM: a shrunk mean trend from the last 20 returns.
  3. FAST:   the recent 5-session trend relative to the 20-session trend.

On projected session d, use:
    expected_log_return[d]
      = slow   * exp(-(d-1)/55)
      + medium * exp(-(d-1)/14)
      + fast   * exp(-(d-1)/4)

The components are bounded; the sum is capped at +/-0.35% per session.
Cumulative expected log-returns transform the most recent authentic close
into future SCENARIO prices. The fast component fades most quickly. This
makes the trajectory bend if recent and longer-run price trends differ.
A genuinely flat trend can quite properly produce a nearly flat forecast.
No sine waves, artificial jagged oscillations or random 'price paths' are
added to make the projection look interesting.

UNCERTAINTY
-----------
An indicative volatility fan uses a multiplicative log-price spread of
1.65 * measured_daily_volatility * sqrt(projected_sessions).
The low/high paths are not empirically calibrated to 80%, 90%, 95%, or any
specified coverage rate. Gaps, earnings, geopolitical news, stock splits,
trading halts and macroeconomic shocks can produce results well outside
these bounds. The model is NOT a probability forecast.

HEADLINES
---------
Finnhub headlines may be shown as sourced context with a simple keyword
classifier. The classifier is not a trained sentiment model and its score
DOES NOT ALTER predicted prices in v1.6.4.

VALIDATION (LIMITED HISTORICAL CHECK)
-------------------------------------
Forecast Lab generates non-overlapping 5-session and 20-session examples.
Each prediction uses only historical closes available at its earlier anchor;
there is no deliberate future-price leakage. Calculate mean absolute
percentage error (MAPE) for the curve's terminal price and for an unchanged-
price baseline using the same dates. Show whichever wins or loses, rather
than reporting only favourable cases. These are backtests of fixed model
assumptions, NOT out-of-sample certification of predictive accuracy;
we have NOT established that the model is generally better than holding
the last observed price, and long-horizon skill is especially uncertain.

TRANSPARENCY / USER INTERFACE
-----------------------------
The real observed chart remains separate from the purple model scenario.
A future hover reads 'SCENARIO, NOT OBSERVED'. A user can disable the overlay.
Provider errors or too little data suppress projected values instead of
manufacturing substitutes. The model has no API-key or extra payment needs
beyond the existing authenticated historical data connection.
