MARKET DESK REBORN v2.4.1 — SIMPLER SIDEBAR

Six main entries replace the long list of 17 tools:

OVERVIEW — your main chart and watchlist.
MARKETS — Stock Browser, Live Tape, Market News and Economy Overview.
INTELLIGENCE — the existing Market Desk AI and statistical overviews.
RESEARCH TOOLS — Company Overview, Compare Companies, Chart Indicators,
Company Valuation, Price Forecasts and Forecast Results.
MY WORKSPACE — Price Alerts, Practice Portfolio, My Research Notes and
Research Reports.
SETTINGS — all existing connections, appearance and backup controls.

Open a group to reveal its tools. One group stays expanded at a time. You can
collapse the current group; its heading still indicates the current section.
Opening a tool from a chart shortcut, module button or quick search reveals
its sidebar group automatically. Overview, Intelligence and Settings condense
the groups again when selected. Groups start compact on a new page load.

QUICK NAVIGATION AND ALERTS
Command+K (Control+K on other keyboards) still searches all 17 tools, including
those inside closed groups. The full names Market Desk Intelligence and
Settings & Checks remain searchable despite the shorter sidebar wording.
Triggered price-alert counts also appear on My workspace, so you can see
that count even when the group's tool list is closed.

KEYBOARD AND MOBILE
Tab to a group heading and press Return or Space to expand/collapse it.
Escape inside an open group collapses it and focuses its heading. Native
browser disclosure controls supply the expanded/collapsed state. The same
menu groups work inside the existing phone menu drawer. Reduced motion,
high contrast, both editions and original rounded styling remain available.

PRESERVED
All 17 tools remain. No charts, AI functions, alerts, notes, reports, saved
research, provider rules or existing settings are removed. No AI model download
or paid service is needed for this navigation change. No private data is in
this release. The previous v2.4 source folder is unchanged.
